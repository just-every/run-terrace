# Reviewed external payment records

The owner records a lease policy with `payment.policy`: tenancyId, current version (0 initially), reference, and terms `{ "stages": [{"minDaysPastDue":0,"allowPartial":true,"overpayment":"reject"}], "priority":["CHARGE_ID"] }`. Each charge appears once in explicit allocation order; all unpaid charges must be included. Later policy revisions preserve prior decisions.

Every assigned active state/local PORT-008 pack must supply `partialPayments.value.stages` in the same stage shape. Missing fields never borrow a default. Admission chooses the last threshold no greater than the age of the oldest outstanding charge on property-local today, separately in the lease and every pack. Every rule must permit the operation. Recording an older external initiation date does not bypass today's stage.

`payment.record` takes tenancyId, amount (USD decimal), state (pending/settled), method (ach/card/cash/check/other), receivedDate, reference, payerKind (resident/co_tenant/housing_authority), and payerId for a co-tenant. A housing-authority record requires the effective approved lease's HAP obligation; it does not prove PAY-013 subsidy reconciliation. Current native forms offer residents/co-tenants only. Pending records reserve exact allocations and credit but reduce no receivable. Reservations, new charges and corrections are fenced in one transaction. A lease with a policy uses this door instead of the legacy single-charge receipt door.

`payment.settle` and `payment.fail` take paymentId, reviewed version, on and an external outcome reference. Settlement turns reserved allocations into receipts exactly once; failure releases the reservation. The original policy and provider/source references remain readable. Excess funds are either refused or retained as separately identified unapplied credit; they are neither allocated to another lease nor automatically applied to future rent, included in owner income, or described as a bank balance. Credit custody accounting and use remain future work.

All new commands currently require the owner; API credentials are denied. This conservative authority default awaits ct approval (PAY-005 stays partial). Nothing here initiates a processor payment, proves bank settlement or sends an instruction. PAY-002/003/004 and PAY-013 must adopt the same admission gate when their collection doors ship. Records follow SAFE-004 accounting retention, subject access, backup and readable export; personal references are redacted after the retention/hold fence.
