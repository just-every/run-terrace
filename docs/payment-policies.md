# Reviewed external payment records

The owner records a lease policy with `payment.policy`: tenancyId, current version (0 initially), reference, and terms `{ "stages": [{"minDaysPastDue":0,"allowPartial":true,"overpayment":"reject"}], "priority":["CHARGE_ID"] }`. Each charge appears once in explicit allocation order; all unpaid charges must be included. Later policy revisions preserve prior decisions.

Every assigned active state/local PORT-008 pack must supply `partialPayments.value.stages` in the same stage shape. Missing fields never borrow a default. Admission chooses the last threshold no greater than the age of the oldest outstanding charge on property-local today, separately in the lease and every pack. Every rule must permit the operation. Recording an older external initiation date does not bypass today's stage.

`payment.record` takes tenancyId, amount (USD decimal), state (pending/settled), method (ach/card/cash/check/other), receivedDate, reference, payerKind (resident/co_tenant/housing_authority), and payerId for a co-tenant. A housing-authority record requires the effective approved lease's HAP obligation; it does not prove PAY-013 subsidy reconciliation. Current native forms offer residents/co-tenants only. Pending records reserve exact allocations and credit but reduce no receivable. Reservations, new charges and corrections are fenced in one transaction. A lease with a policy uses this door instead of the legacy single-charge receipt door.

`payment.settle` and `payment.fail` take paymentId, reviewed version, on and an external outcome reference. Settlement turns reserved allocations into receipts exactly once; failure releases the reservation. The original policy and provider/source references remain readable. Excess funds are either refused or retained as separately identified unapplied credit; they are neither allocated to another lease nor automatically applied to future rent, included in owner income, or described as a bank balance. Credit custody accounting and use remain future work.

All new commands currently require the owner; API credentials are denied. This conservative authority default awaits ct approval (PAY-005 stays partial). Nothing here initiates a processor payment, proves bank settlement or sends an instruction. PAY-002/003/004 and PAY-013 must adopt the same admission gate when their collection doors ship. Records follow SAFE-004 accounting retention, subject access, backup and readable export; personal references are redacted after the retention/hold fence.

## Late-fee schedules

Owner-only `latefee.policy` takes tenancyId, chargeId (a confirmed USD monthly-rent source), current version, reference and terms: fullUnpaidDays, feeCents, capCents, weekendRule (calendar/next_business_day), excludeTimely (boolean). Values are whole integer cents/days. The native form accepts decimal USD and translates it exactly. A fee charge can never become a fee basis. Each active state/local pack must explicitly state its lateFee monthly_rent basis and cap fields plus weekendRule, holidays (ISO days), pendingPolicy (exclude_timely/count_pending) and reversalPolicy (hold/recalculate_once); no default is substituted.

`latefee.preview` takes tenancyId and on (elapsed property-local schedule day), returning calculation lines and a fingerprint. `latefee.post` takes the same fields and fingerprint. A changed balance, reservation, source policy or pack requires a new review; replay never duplicates a source fee. Timely pending allocations exclude no more than their reserved principal; settlement after grace does not retrospectively create a fee. A return under a hold policy requires human resolution. Existing fees always recalculate under their original pack/lease snapshot, and can only be reduced, never compounded. Paid/reserved fee reductions need reviewed refund/reallocation. `latefee.waive` takes feeId, amount, on, reason and unique reference; the original charge and policy remain. Existing balanced-journal triggers carry each charge and reduction. The native ledger shows calculations and waivers at 1440 and 390.

PAY-006 stays partial pending ct approval of owner-only fee configuration, reviewed manual schedule execution and waiver authority. No active legal values ship. Synthetic tests represent owner counsel inputs and make no Terrace counsel-review claim.

## Approved external refunds and corrections

`finance.prepare` takes kind (refund/credit/write_off), amount (USD), on, reason and reference. A refund additionally takes receiptId, payee and method (original/ach/check); the original method must be recorded, and a check needs forwardingAddress and the externally assigned bank checkNumber (digits). A credit/write-off takes chargeId. The response contains id, version, fingerprint and exact before/after resident and owner effects. `finance.revise` takes actionId, current version and complete replacement fields; it invalidates prior approval. Recorded actions cannot be edited.

`finance.approve` takes actionId, version and fingerprint. Only the owner or in-scope portfolio manager may prepare, approve or record. ROLE-003 applies: preparers cannot approve with another active eligible approver; a sole operator is labelled. The source's available balance, pending reservations and exact owner effects must still match. Approval reserves refund capacity against the source receipt. A revised source needs new review, even if the amount is unchanged. Prior approvals remain in history. The source never changes in place.

`finance.record` takes the approved actionId/version/fingerprint, unique externalReference and confirm: RECORDED. It requires Base's fresh email-link proof; live approver authority and portfolio scope are rechecked. Refunds are staff-recorded external facts. Repeating the same reference returns the original result without another ledger reversal; a different reference or stale source is refused. Partial refund cash follows the original receipt owners, including after ownership transfers. Exact cents are floored proportionally; the largest original allocation gets remainder cents, owner id on a tie. An already partially refunded receipt cannot also be fully reversed; refund its remaining amount instead. Credits/write-offs reduce an unreserved unpaid charge with the recorded reason and posting-day owner allocation already used by the adjustment journal.

`finance.check` registers the approved numbered forwarding-address draft; an unchanged draft is reused. A revised draft never reuses the old reserved number. A draft is non-negotiable: Terrace prints/mails no check. After checking an actual externally issued check, staff may record that fact through finance.record; the register distinguishes the two states and never claims bank clearance. No provider, ACH or check instruction is sent. PAY-009 stays partial for actual provider execution/reconciliation, ACCT-017 negotiable check issuance and ACCT-018 ACH acceptance, plus ct approval of this conservative authority/external-record default. These future rails inherit the same exact approval and once-only source limit.

Financial action details and forwarding addresses are visible only to the owner and scoped portfolio managers; ordinary ledger readers receive no action details. Subject access, SAFE-004 accounting retention/redaction, backup and readable export apply. New database guards make every existing receipt/reduction door, including concessions and close corrections, respect pending allocations.

Late-fee eligibility reconstructs unpaid principal at the first eligible fee day (or earlier schedule day). A later payment of late principal does not erase an earned fee. Successful delayed settlement of a timely initiated payment keeps its protection; a failed timely pending payment follows the explicit reversal policy. The first posting also fences the structure unit count used for its cap. Restores reject inconsistent payment allocations, reservations, refunds, approvals and frozen late-fee amounts before any product data is installed.

## Resident monthly statements (PAY-010 partial)

A full resident grant opens `/portal/statement?tenancyId=ID&month=YYYY-MM&format=html|csv|json` from the native lease account. Months end on their calendar last day, or property-local today in the current month. Future months are refused. Opening and closing receivables follow dated charge, net receipt/refund and reduction events known now; reductions use their approved posting day when supplied. Current recorded balance is shown separately from the month closing, along with as-of pending external funds and settled unapplied credit. Neither pending funds nor credit are automatically deducted. A projection is not a sent invoice.

`/portal/transaction?tenancyId=ID&id=SOURCE` inspects one of the statement's own opaque source ids (JSON with format=json). It shows only the financial kind, day, amount and matching charge already readable on that lease. Other household contacts, arbitrary staff notes and private bank/provider references remain excluded. Only full residents reach these new doors. Delegated contacts keep their existing balance/charges CSV; they receive no monthly payment/source details. Every request rechecks the live grant and session. No portal credential is archived or reactivated by restore.

Accessible HTML and Unicode CSV ship; accessible PDF, delivered receipts/invoices and failed-email follow-up tasks remain open. Unsupported PDF is explicitly refused. No automatic send is added. Staff delinquency JSON/CSV/native views now show matching dated external pending funds separately, and correctly reconstruct linked partial refunds and approved correction dates.
