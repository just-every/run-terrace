What is Tenant Screening?
Tenant screening is the process of evaluating and qualifying a prospective tenant by looking at their credit history, criminal record, employment status, rental history and more.
What are the benefits of using tenant screening software?
When it comes to filling vacancies with qualified residents, Buildium’s Tenant Screening software can do a lot, including:
Increase efficiency by automating the screening process, which saves you a significant amount of time and effort.
Improve accuracy using reliable data sources to verify a prospective tenant’s information, resulting in fewer errors.
Identifying residents who are more likely to pay rent on time and take care of the property.
Reduce risk by raising red flags on questionable applicants with criminal records or prior evictions.
Ensure a process that complies with fair housing laws and other regulations, minimizing your exposure.
How much does tenant screening cost?
The cost of tenant screening normally varies and depends on several factors, including the type of screening and the screening provider.
With Buildium, Tenant Screening costs $17 per screen (or $35 per screen if you pass the cost to the applicant) and Enhanced Tenant Screening is $20 per screen.
How long does it take to get results from a tenant background check?
In general, most background checks take 2-7 business days to complete, although some may take longer. The tenant initiates the process by submitting their information and consent to a background check.
With Buildium, reports are typically generated within 24 hours of the applicant completing the screening process, some within minutes.
What does a rental background check consist of?
A rental background check includes a credit check, criminal background check, and eviction history report, based on records available through our screening provider’s databases. Coverage varies by state and county, and local or federal law may limit what’s reportable.
How can I use Tenant Screening to find good tenants?
There are several steps you can take to find good tenants with tenant screening, including:
Creating a tenant screening checklist: Create a list of criteria that potential tenants must meet in order to qualify for your rental property.
Requiring an application: Have tenants to fill out a rental application that includes their contact information, employment history, rental history, and references.
Checking credit reports: Run a credit check on potential tenants to evaluate their creditworthiness.
Verifying employment: Contact the tenant’s employer to verify their employment status and income level.
Contacting previous landlords: Review rental history, including their payment history, any damages they caused to the property.
Conducting background checks: Check for criminal records, evictions, and other red flags.
