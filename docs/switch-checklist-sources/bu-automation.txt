How can automations improve efficiency for my Property Management Company?
Automation eliminates manual bottlenecks so your team can focus on growth and resident satisfaction.
Here’s how it drives efficiency:
Time Savings: Automate rent reminders, task assignment, and owner reports to reclaim hours every week.
Error Reduction: Automated checklists and workflows ensure compliance and accuracy, reducing costly mistakes.
Better Communication: Trigger personalized messages and updates instantly, keeping residents and owners informed without extra effort.
Scalability: As your portfolio grows, automation handles the volume without adding headcount–perfect for small teams and large operations alike.
What’s basic vs. advanced automation in Buildium?
Basic Automation:
Online rent collection and recurring payments
Automated accounting workflows (bank reconciliations, 1099 filing)
Standard email notifications and reminders
Advanced Automation:
Workflow Automations for multi-step processes
AI-powered tools like Write with AI for drafting emails and announcements
Lumina AI Workforce—goal-driven agents that proactively manage leasing, maintenance, and reporting
AI Bill Scan for instant invoice processing
Open API access for custom integrations.
How can I start automating tasks in Buildium now?
Getting started is simple. Buildium Includes basic automation like payment reminders, automated late fees, and scheduled payments for residents so you can offload repetitive tasks.
For more advanced needs, Growth and Premium plans unlock custom workflows and API integrations for deeper automation.
Is automation available in every plan?
Yes—every Buildium plan includes core automation to simplify your day-to-day operations, such as automated accounting workflows, online payments, and task management. However, advanced automation features vary by plan:
Essential: Covers the basics—automated bookkeeping, payment processing, and standard reporting—so you can manage operations from a central platform.
Growth: Adds enhanced automation like Reporting Automation and customizable Workflow Automations.
Premium: Unlocks the most advanced automation capabilities, including Lumina AI Workforce—goal-driven agents that proactively manage leasing, maintenance, and accounting workflows with zero manual setup—plus Open API access for custom integrations.
