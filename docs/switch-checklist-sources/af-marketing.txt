Frequently asked questions
What marketing and leasing capabilities does AppFolio offer?
AppFolio includes a full suite of marketing and leasing capabilities that cover every stage of the leasing funnel, from listing syndication and lead management to tenant screening and lease signing, all within a single connected system. Property managers can price units, market to prospects across major rental platforms, automate lead nurturing, and execute the full leasing workflow without switching between tools. These capabilities are built into AppFolio and designed to work together, giving teams centralized visibility into marketing and leasing performance across their entire portfolio.
Book a personalized demo to learn more.
Does AppFolio automatically syndicate rental listings to third-party sites?
Yes. AppFolio automatically syndicates rental listings to major rental platforms including Zillow, Apartments.com, and Zumper directly from within the system, eliminating manual re-entry across sites. Property managers maintain and update listings in one place, and changes are reflected across syndicated platforms automatically. AppFolio also integrates with Engrain for interactive map-based search and Blue Moon for lease document management.
Get a personalized demo to learn more.
What is AppFolio Realm-X Leasing Performer?
AppFolio Realm-X Leasing Performer is an agentic AI leasing capability built into AppFolio that autonomously manages prospect communication throughout the lead-to-lease process. It responds to every inbound lead within minutes, 24 hours a day, schedules tours, sends personalized follow-ups, and updates guest cards without requiring manual action from leasing staff. The Leasing Performer supports both onsite and offsite leasing teams, making it suitable for mixed portfolios, and works alongside AppFolio's Leasing CRM to ensure smooth handoffs between automated and human-led interactions.
Get a personalized demo to learn more.
How does AppFolio's Leasing CRM work?
AppFolio's Leasing CRM is a lead management capability built into AppFolio that tracks every prospect across a portfolio through a single guest card, from first inquiry through lease signing. It generates automated, prioritized to-do lists for leasing teams with clear calls to action, so staff can respond to leads faster, book more tours, and close more leases. Leasing managers can configure the CRM to reflect their own best practices and use it to monitor pipeline progress and team performance across all properties.
Book a personalized demo to learn more.
Does AppFolio include tenant screening?
Yes. AppFolio includes FolioScreen Trusted Renter, an integrated tenant screening capability that combines background checks, criminal history, income verification, employment verification, and ID verification within the same system used to manage the rest of the leasing process. FolioScreen is designed to support Fair Housing compliance and includes fraud detection tools to help property managers identify risk before approving an applicant. Because screening is built directly into AppFolio, there is no need to export applicant data to a third-party service.
Book a personalized demo to learn more.
Can AppFolio manage leasing across a large portfolio with multiple property types?
AppFolio is designed for portfolio scale and supports leasing operations across both onsite and offsite teams, making it suitable for managers who oversee mixed or geographically distributed portfolios. The Leasing CRM creates a single guest card for every lead that tracks their activity across all properties in the portfolio, giving leasing teams and leadership a consolidated view of pipeline health. Customers managing portfolios of 1,500 units or more use AppFolio's marketing and leasing workflows alongside its accounting, maintenance, and reporting capabilities within the same platform.
How does AppFolio help property managers reduce vacancy rates?
AppFolio reduces vacancy rates by reducing the time between lead generation and lease signing through a combination of automated lead response, pricing tools, and integrated leasing workflows. AppFolio Realm-X Leasing Performer responds to every lead within minutes and keeps prospects engaged throughout the process, while Leasing Signals helps property managers set competitive, market-informed rent prices to attract qualified applicants faster. Together, these capabilities reduce the gaps in the leasing funnel where prospects typically drop off, such as slow follow-up, mispriced units, and manual screening delays.
How does AppFolio's Leasing Signals work?
AppFolio's Leasing Signals capability gives property managers a data-backed foundation for making pricing decisions. Rather than relying on a single input, Leasing Signals weighs a range of factors, including your occupancy goals, unit turn costs, and lease expiration timing, to surface pricing guidance tailored to your portfolio and strategy. Managers can accept, modify, or override suggestions based on local knowledge, keeping teams in control without requiring them to manually reconstruct that knowledge for every unit or renewal. By grounding renewal pricing in a full picture of portfolio conditions, Leasing Signals supports the kind of offers that keep satisfied residents from shopping alternatives.
Your strategy, goals, data, and pricing decisions are used exclusively for your business and kept confidential. Your proprietary information is never factored into anyone else's pricing. Leasing Signals is available as an add-on or included in AppFolio Performance Platform Max.*
*Leasing Signals that aggregate public data are not available in all jurisdictions.

How does AppFolio support tenant lead generation for rental properties?
AppFolio supports tenant lead generation by syndicating listings to high-traffic rental platforms including Zillow, Apartments.com, and Zumper directly from within the system, maximizing the reach of each vacancy without manual reposting across sites. Once leads are generated, AppFolio's Realm-X Leasing Performer automatically responds to every inquiry within minutes, ensuring no prospect goes uncontacted. All inbound leads are captured and tracked in the Leasing CRM through a single guest card per prospect, giving leasing teams a complete, real-time view of their pipeline from first touch through lease signing.
Book a personalized demo to learn more.
Can AppFolio help property managers identify and target the right renter demographics for their marketing?
AppFolio's Leasing Signals capability helps property managers align their pricing and marketing strategy to local market conditions by aggregating comparable public data on nearby units, giving teams insight into what similar properties are renting for and how competitive their units are positioned. This market context helps managers attract the right renter segment for each property type and price point. For portfolio-level marketing performance, AppFolio provides centralized reporting on lead sources, pipeline activity, and leasing outcomes, helping teams understand which marketing channels and unit types are converting and adjust their strategy accordingly.
