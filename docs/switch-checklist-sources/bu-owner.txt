Frequently Asked Questions
What is a property owner portal?
A property owner portal lets you give property owners 24/7 access to critical resources, reducing phone calls and emails. You can share financial reports in real time, collect payments online, share important documents and more—from anywhere.
What is the difference between the Resident Center portal and the Property Owner Portal?
The Resident Center is a portal for your residents that has everything they need to communicate with you, submit and track maintenance requests, submit payments, and receive community updates and stay connected. The Property Owner Portal is similar—it is dedicated for your rental owner clients to stay connected with you via a digital and mobile portal that they can use to fund contribution requests and self-serve answers to questions they have about reports, finances, and documents.
What are the benefits of having a property owner portal?
With a property owner portal, owners of rental properties are able to see up-to-date financials and reporting, resolve outstanding tasks, approve invoices, fund contribution requests, and access important documents—24/7 from any device, anywhere.
What methods can I use to distribute payments through the property owners portal?
Payments to rental owners can be made via EFT or by check.
Can I share property maintenance and repair records through the property owner portal?
Yes! A property owner is able to view the complete history of maintenance and repair tasks completed within their portfolio of properties.
Can property owners use the portal for multiple properties?
Property owners can use the Owner Portal to access all of their properties in one place.
