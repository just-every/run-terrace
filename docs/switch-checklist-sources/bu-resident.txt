Frequently Asked Questions
What is Resident Center?
The Buildium Resident Center gives your residents an effortless experience with 24/7 online access to the most important services including paying fees or rent, submitting and tracking maintenance requests, and staying in sync with text and email updates.
How is Resident Center different from a tenant portal or a resident portal?
It’s not! The Buildium Resident Center is essentially a comprehensive tenant portal. From a single place, residents get 24/7 online access to the most important services, including paying fees or rent, submitting and tracking maintenance requests, and staying in sync with text and email updates.
How can Resident Center help me streamline my operations?
Buildium’s Resident Center streamlines operations by offering a centralized place for everything tenants or homeowners need, including paying fees or rent, submitting and tracking maintenance requests, accessing lease documents, buying renters insurance and more.
Can Resident Center be whitelabeled to my business?
Resident Center lets you put your brand front and center with custom logos and colors to help create a cohesive online experience and build trust with your residents.
Does Resident Center have a mobile app?
Yes! The highly-rated Buildium Resident Center mobile app is available for download on iOS and Android. In fact, Buildium’s mobile apps set it apart from the competition.
What can tenants do on Resident Center?
A lot! The Buildium Resident Center gives your residents an effortless experience with 24/7 online access to the most important services including paying fees or rent, submitting and tracking maintenance requests, and staying in sync with text and email updates.
Is Resident Center secure?
Protecting the personal and financial information of residents is a critical priority for Buildium. All customer data is stored with Amazon RDS Encryption, which ensures that everything is encrypted at rest, including the underlying storage for the database, as well as any data in backups or replicas.
