# Designated bank custody

Open **Rent & arrears → Open balanced property ledger → Trust accounts and beneficiary balances**. This is a record of reviewed external USD facts. Terrace stores account labels, bank names, last four digits and legal-entity references, never full bank numbers or bank credentials. It does not move money, query a bank or verify a statement. ACCT-006 owns bank-statement reconciliation; ACCT-010 owns owner payout execution.

The owner records a designated **operating**, **rent trust** or **security deposit** account under an existing legal entity, then authorizes it for a property with an external authorization reference. All authorized accounts for a property belong to one legal entity. A deposit account is authorized only for deposit funds; operating and rent-trust accounts only for operating funds. Each property/fund has at most one immutable primary account for new cash. An account’s designation and recorded references cannot be rewritten. For now corrections to configuration need a reviewed product change; no automatic remapping or destructive replacement exists.

Each new journal cash source is assigned atomically to its primary bank, retaining its original owner, property, fund, day and source. A rent receipt requires a rent-trust primary if one is configured; deposits remain liabilities and use their deposit primary. A missing mapping never invents custody. Historical sources and sources entered without a primary remain explicit unassigned exceptions. The owner may assign each source to a compatible authorized bank without editing its financial entry. Transfers are held until every cash source for their property/fund has a reviewed assignment. Unlinked historical journal sources require a separately reviewed migration; this door cannot assign them to a new property or owner.

Balances are exact cents for each **bank + property + beneficiary + fund**, so a positive balance elsewhere cannot cover an overdraft. The reconciliation shows each property/beneficiary/fund’s general-ledger cash, assigned-bank cash and unassigned difference, and names every unassigned source even if the net difference is zero. These are internally reconciled recorded balances, not verified bank balances. A source reversal retains its original bank custody and cannot overdraw it after cash has been transferred. Original sources, mappings, assignments, approvals and external transfer receipts are immutable.

## Reviewed external transfers

A scoped bookkeeper or portfolio manager prepares one immutable amount/day/reason from one authorized bank to another. Both banks must belong to the same property, beneficiary, fund, legal entity and exact designated purpose. Inter-owner and inter-property transfers, crossings between operating/rent-trust/deposit purposes, and owner payouts through this door are refused. A deposit-funded owner payout is refused before any execution. The refusal remains in the scoped custody exception history.

The source account cannot borrow from later-dated receipts or become negative on an intervening recorded day. It must retain the beneficiary’s share of the management agreement’s reserve in force on the transfer day. It uses PORT-003’s ownership split: floor each share in cents, remainder to the largest share, ties to its earlier position. The reserve floor is applied separately to each source bank; it is not a claim that reserves have been verified at the bank. A dated agreement and owner share are required. Deposit transfers retain zero operating reserve and cannot change their liability or beneficiary. The day must already have occurred in the property’s time zone and cannot be in a closed month.

An owner or scoped portfolio manager approves the exact prepared terms under ROLE-003. The preparer cannot approve if another active approver exists; the sole-operator exception is visibly labeled **self-approved: no second person**. Approval posts no money and sends no instruction. A scoped bookkeeper/portfolio manager then explicitly records the external bank reference once. Fresh email-link confirmation is required for account creation, mapping, historical assignment, approval and external recording. At recording, live actor and approver authority, ownership, agreement, reserve and bank balance are rechecked; concurrent attempts cannot overspend. A changed reserve or revoked approver holds recording. Preparations do not reserve money and cannot be used as bank instructions. Both custody sides post together; the total beneficiary fund and its general ledger stay unchanged. Recorded transfers cannot be edited or replayed.

Configuration and historical assignment are owner-only; accountants can read scoped balances through existing financial-report authority; members, leasing and maintenance cannot use these doors. Installation API credentials cannot access custody or run any trust command. Native backup and readable export include custody records. Staged restore checks designated boundaries, source links, human approval and bank beneficiary solvency before installing records, and does not send or replay a payment.
