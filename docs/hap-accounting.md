# Voucher payer accounting

Record the signed HAP obligation on the lease version, including the named PHA,
contract reference, source clause and exact resident and PHA portions per rent
cadence. Approve the signed version before posting the ordinary rent run. The
two portions remain separate receivables and preserve the combined gross cents,
including prorating. Historical shared charges retain their recorded identity;
Terrace does not reconstruct a split that was never recorded.

In Documents, upload the original as **PHA notice or remittance**. These are
accounting records with seven-year fiscal retention, legal holds and an original
fingerprint. Existing document upload authority applies; scoped ledger readers
can read PHA accounting evidence. Tax and screening rights remain separate.

In Rent & arrears, open **Record an external PHA remittance**. Record the named
PHA, ACH or check, exact amount, external reference, date and stored remittance
detail. This records documentary evidence of money handled elsewhere. It does
not originate a payment or verify a bank settlement. A pending remittance
reserves explicitly named allocations without posting cash or reducing debt.
Record its received or failed outcome from external evidence once.

Allocate only the charge amounts named in the remittance detail. A lump sum can
cover several leases, properties and owners; its complete identity and held
remainder require scope over every linked tenancy. A remaining unmatched amount
stays held without an inferred owner, collection or automatic use. Wrong payers,
duplicate details, excessive amounts and stale versions are refused. Receipt
journals preserve each charge's beneficiary despite a later ownership change.

Use **Record a PHA split change or abatement** for a retroactive PHA notice.
Preparation and preview post nothing. Review the exact correction and have
another eligible approver approve it; an installation with no second approver
uses the existing explicitly labelled solo exception. A split changes the
portions from its effective date and governs later runs of those signed terms
until another approved split or signed version replaces it. The end date names
the posted periods being reconciled. Original signed sources remain available.
Corrections retain the prior entitlement periods and post on the approval's
property-local day, so an earlier as-of report does not backdate the correction.
Pending allocations prevent a correction from consuming their reserved balance.

An inspection abatement reverses only the PHA entitlement for the explicitly
reviewed month. It creates no resident charge and does not silently restore
subsidy in a later split review. A correction to paid entitlement exposes a
separate payer credit; it authorizes no refund or transfer.

**Payer and owner reconciliation** shows separate resident and PHA debt and
credits, each beneficiary's receivable and externally recorded cash, the
reconciliation difference and a CSV download. Resident and owner portals retain
their existing subject and ownership boundaries. Resident monthly statements
include only the resident side of a correction. Delinquency reconstructs dated
receipts and corrections separately by payer; PHA debt never enters resident
late-fee eligibility.

Cash scenarios show dated resident and PHA charges, receipts, corrections,
abatement, pending funds, held funds and outcomes as separate source activity.
These actuals do not become an assumed opening bank balance or a promise of
future collection. Missing actuals retain explicit forecast assumptions.

Native archives and readable exports carry the financial evidence and originals.
Restoration grants no payment or outbound authority. Eligible reviewed erasure
withdraws originals and personal text, preserves exact amounts under opaque
identities, and fences an older archive and identical original reupload. Shared
accounting holds apply to every linked subject; shared remittance references
remain until the final subject's accounting identity is eligible and erased.
