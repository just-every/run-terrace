# Office printing and recorded service

In Messages, prepare a notice for its named recipient and approve its exact content. Open **Office printing**, enter the preparation reference and choose whether a building announcement includes all its approved recipients. Terrace freezes each recipient's letter, a combined PDF and a manifest. Each recipient begins on a separate page. The letter uses the recorded tenancy address for residents and household notice recipients, or the recorded owner's mailing address. A vendor without a recorded postal address cannot be printed.

Review the frozen recipient and batch PDFs, then approve their displayed SHA-256 hashes and manifest with a review reference. Downloading an approved PDF returns those frozen bytes. Changed content, original files, recipients or addresses require new preparation and approval. A withdrawn recipient grant immediately stops print access. A prepared file, reviewed PDF, approved print or download records no service, sends nothing and starts no legal deadline.

Record the external service or attempt separately. Supply the actual server's name and identity or organization reference, actual date and time with the property's UTC offset, method, evidence reference and original PNG or JPEG photograph. Office certified mail also requires its tracking number. The actual time and the time staff recorded it are kept separately. Future times, incorrect property offsets and mismatched service dates are refused.

Delivered, failed, uncertain and returned records remain distinct. A failed, uncertain or returned attempt opens an assigned review due on the property-local recording date. Reconcile that review with independent evidence, a responsible person, next action and due date. Reconciliation preserves the original attempt and never retries a send. A photograph passes only the existing static content check; it receives no malware verdict.

The native API uses `thread.print.prepare` with `threadId`, `version`, `fingerprint`, `reference` and optional `allRecipients=1`. `thread.print.approve` accepts `printBatchId`, `manifestSha256`, `pdfSha256`, `reference` and `consent=APPROVE EXACT PRINT`. Both require a signed-in owner or currently scoped portfolio manager with fresh email-link proof. `GET /api/thread-print?id=<batch>` returns the approved combined PDF; `item=<item>` selects its recipient PDF and `manifest=1` selects the manifest. `review=1` permits inline review before approval. These reads still check current staff scope and recipient authority.

`thread.service` retains its exact notice version, fingerprint and `consent=RECORD EVIDENCE`. Its multipart fields include `outcome`, `method`, `reference`, `occurredAt` (for example `2026-10-27T08:30-07:00`), `serverName`, `serverReference`, `photo` and optional `servedAt`, `tracking` and `printBatchId`. A supplied service date must agree with the actual timestamp. A linked print batch must have current exact approval. `GET /api/thread-service-photo?id=<thread>` downloads the staff-only original with private cache controls.

Communication retention and legal holds apply to PDFs, approvals, attempts and photographs. A communication or all-record legal hold on any tenancy in a shared batch prevents erasure through another tenancy; retention review names the shared hold without revealing the other subject or their reason. Backup and readable export retain the originals and attribution. A restored batch is evidence only: it grants no print or service authority. Erasure removes combined PDFs and manifests containing the erased tenancy, that tenancy's recipient copies and its service photographs, and records their non-resurrection fingerprints. Other tenancies keep their frozen recipient PDFs and original hash approval receipts under their own retention periods and holds. A retired batch exposes only those retained recipient originals and cannot authorize a new service record; prepare and approve a new batch for that. Restoring an older archive requires the later erasure journal.

This office workflow does not qualify statutory service, notice eligibility, pack-based deadlines or mailing extensions. Counsel-reviewed PORT-008 content, ACCT-004 deposit disposition and Lob account, entitlement and provider events remain outstanding. Existing communication permission and approval rules remain in force.
