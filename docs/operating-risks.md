# Operating-risk observations

The installation owner opens **Properties → Analyze operating risks** to run an
on-demand documentary analysis. Select a property, its recorded HVAC equipment
and an inclusive property-local date window. The window is a **job preparation
cohort**: jobs prepared outside it are excluded. Attempt effective dates and
recording dates, and resident-check recording dates, must be on or before its
end. Current job status and approved terms are separately labelled snapshots.

Each selected asset keeps its actual identifier, repair job identifiers,
completion attempt identifiers, callback classifications and resident checks.
An asset needs at least two recorded callbacks to show repeated callbacks.
Other assets never contribute to that count. Completion and resident outcomes
are staff records; an unresolved or unanswered check is not a confirmation.
Missing sources remain visible as insufficient evidence.

Leasing observations reuse the leasing report's first-contact cohorts and
first-source attribution. Held and merged duplicates are excluded; reviewed
separate prospects remain distinct. Showing-to-application drop-off counts
prospects with a recorded showing and no recorded application. Application-to-
lease drop-off counts prospects with a recorded application and no recorded
lease. Each percentage uses its own stage denominator, and a zero denominator
is not measurable. Both effective event dates and property-local recording
dates must be on or before the window end. Skipped stages are never inferred.
These are observations, not independent verification or causal findings.

Optional owner-entered USD assumptions require a date, source reference, exact
preventive cost, exact cost per avoided callback and a whole assumed avoided
callback count. Gross benefit is that count multiplied by the assumed callback
cost; net benefit subtracts assumed preventive cost. These values are not a
recommended plan, measured probability or realized saving. Current approved
repair estimates are grouped by currency and never substituted for settled
costs or callback charges. Settled cost and realized savings remain unknown.

Every explicit analysis reads current authorized native sources again. The
analysis persists no source copy, workload, proposal, approval or credential,
and makes no model or provider request. It grants no authority to change a
vendor, budget, fee, contact or workflow. Preventive-plan approval, activation,
continuing stewardship and later actual-versus-forecast comparison remain owed,
as do broader operating risks beyond these callback and cohort observations.
API credentials and all non-owner roles are refused. Existing sessions are
checked live by the native session door; revoked sessions cannot read sources.
