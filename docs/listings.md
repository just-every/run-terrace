# Own-site vacancies

The installation owner opens Prospects, then **Manage vacancy listings**. Choose a unit, enter the public title and description, exact USD monthly rent, fees and deposit, and an available date. Saving creates an immutable revision. Preview that revision, check the displayed amounts, and explicitly publish it. A later draft leaves the previously approved public revision unchanged until the owner publishes its exact preview. Withdrawal removes the public listing and keeps its dated history.

The public vacancy directory is at `/vacancies`. Each current listing has a text-only page and an enquiry form that works on a phone. Only approved marketing fields are public. Current or future leases, nonrentable units and changed inventory suppress publication and invalidate prior previews. The owner must prepare a current revision before publishing again.

Visitors provide a name, an email address or phone number, and explicit contact preferences for each channel. Terrace resolves the property from the currently published listing and retains the exact listing revision. Each submission creates an unverified own-site prospect; a matching open contact is held for staff duplicate review. The receipt exposes no existing contact, duplicate status or prospect identifier. Retrying an accepted request does not create another prospect. Enquiries send no message and create no application, fee, payment or invitation.

The leasing report keeps actual publication, price change, withdrawal and availability events alongside current checked own-site freshness. Zillow and Apartments.com remain unconfigured, with unknown partner freshness. Ready inventory alone never establishes a listing.

Frozen revisions, publication history and enquiry attribution are retained in backups and readable exports. Restores are unpublished and require fresh owner previews and publication. Prospect records follow the existing communications retention and owner-request erasure policy; retained applications prevent erasure, and the later erasure journal fences older backups. Erased request identifiers cannot recreate the submission.

This text-only slice does not qualify legal fee or deposit disclosures, marketing photos or floorplans, delegated-manager publication, partner submission and synchronization, or enquiry-to-application actions. Existing counsel, applicant authority, provider and custody requirements remain open.
