# Maintenance from request to reconciled bill: exact terms

The exact terms of the clauses in the “Maintenance from request to reconciled bill” area of [CONTRACT.md](../../../CONTRACT.md). Each clause there is the requirement; the terms below define it.

## WORK-001 — Triage safety and emergency requests

Exact semantics (19 October 2026, ct ruling 16:58Z): staff (owner, portfolio manager, maintenance coordinator in the property’s portfolios) record a safety report on a tenancy placed on a unit, with the risk (gas, fire, electric, water, habitability, other), how it arrived (phone, email, in person, observed by staff), who reported it, what was reported, an optional in-service asset, the reported-at instant (an ISO time with a UTC offset, never in the future; blank is now) and their own explicit emergency yes or no; gas, fire and electrical hazards, and any risk whose current guidance is marked immediate danger, are recorded only as emergencies. The owner or a portfolio manager over every portfolio approves numbered guidance versions per risk; immediate-danger guidance must carry a when-to-call instruction naming 911 (and the utility where it applies); each case keeps the exact text of the version it was given, and a risk with no approved guidance records that none was given. The owner or a portfolio manager in scope names one on-call person and one backup per portfolio (an active owner, or staff whose role may acknowledge emergencies there); the latest assignment applies to new cases. Terrace keeps no office hours: on call applies at every hour. Recording an emergency emails the on-call person a sign-in link to the case with no case details through the installation’s mail provider, and the case history records the provider’s own outcome (accepted by the mail provider, failed, uncertain, not sent because development or demo mail is suppressed, or not sent because no provider is configured); “accepted” never claims a person was reached. A portfolio with no on-call person records the case and says no alert was sent. Escalation is a recorded human action by those staff, with a reason, re-sending the alert to the backup or the on-call person; nothing escalates on a timer. Only the on-call person or backup named on the case acknowledges it, in Terrace, saying what they are doing; an acknowledged case stays open. Only the owner or a portfolio manager resolves, after acknowledgement, with field-work evidence and the resident’s follow-up, labeled “resident follow-up recorded by staff; Terrace did not contact the resident”. Members and preparers cannot record, acknowledge or resolve, and no agent or automatic path classifies or closes a case. REPORT-004 counts each emergency in scope as open, with its age in hours (one decimal) from the reported-at instant, until it is resolved. Case records are erased with their tenancy as maintenance jobs are (SAFE-004) and are in the backup and readable export (SAFE-005). Resident self-report uses the same case, approved guidance and on-call email through PORTAL-004. Separate exact-content human approvals now submit manual phone pages through PORTAL-005; provider completion never acknowledges the case. Nothing escalates on a timer under the existing ruling.

## WORK-002 — Maintain property asset and warranty history

Exact semantics: an asset is recorded under a tenancy and, when that tenancy is on a unit (PORT-002), under the unit too, so it shows and takes repairs on every tenancy of that unit; erasing the tenancy it was recorded under erases it. Its manual is recorded as a link or the place it is kept; PORT-007 adds stored manual files. A replacement is recorded as a new asset installed on the replacement date; the old asset is marked replaced on that date, linked both ways, and keeps its service history. A repair request (a maintenance job) links to one in-service asset of the same tenancy; a replaced asset takes no new requests. A linked repair is attributed to the warranty when its due date falls on or after the installed date and on or before the warranty end (inclusive), otherwise to the property owner; Terrace flags coverage and never files or confirms a warranty claim. Costs are approved estimates in the tenancy’s currency (a prepared, unapproved job is listed but not counted); WORK-009 links matched invoices and REPORT-004 the failure analytics, and PORTAL-004 resident requests inherit the asset link before they ship.

## WORK-003 — Estimate work and obtain owner approval

Exact semantics: any operator who prepares work records quotes on a job (a directory vendor or a named unlisted one, quoted scope, available-from date, price before tax and tax, in the job’s currency) and compares them by total, price plus tax, lowest first; selecting a quote makes its vendor, scope and total the job’s terms. An owner or admin records the property owner’s per-job spending limit and optional yearly maintenance budget, labeled “recorded by staff for the owner”, for the property when the tenancy is on a unit (PORT-002), otherwise for the tenancy. With no limit recorded a manager’s approval stands for any amount. A job needs the owner’s decision when its estimate is above the limit, or when the estimates of approved and handed-off jobs in the same scope due in the same calendar year as this job, plus this estimate, are above the budget. Until the owner portal exists (PORTAL-002), an owner or admin records that decision (approved or declined) with its source (email, document or call) and where it is recorded, labeled “recorded by staff for the owner”; Terrace never claims it asked or notified the owner, and members and agents cannot record it. The decision covers the exact terms of one job version (title, scope, vendor, due date, estimate and quote, by SHA-256); approval of a job that needs it requires an approved decision on those exact terms, and the approval snapshot and handoff packet name it. Any change of scope, title, estimate, due date or selected quote, before or after approval, makes a new version that is prepared again (WORK-004 qualification is rechecked): the earlier approval and owner decision no longer apply, the packet cannot be downloaded and the handoff cannot be recorded until the new terms are approved. PORTAL-002 makes owners decide in the portal, and WORK-005, WORK-006, WORK-009 and ROLE-003’s per-person approval limits inherit these terms before they ship (ROLE-001 portfolio managers approve within the owner’s limit and budget recorded here).

## WORK-004 — Qualify and assign vendors

Exact semantics: a vendor qualifies for a job when it is in the directory and not blocked, lists the job’s trade as a specialty, names a service area (a ZIP code or city) that appears as a whole word in the property address, has a received W-9, and has insurance, and any recorded license, expiring on or after the job’s due date. The W-9 status, insurance and license dates are what a manager records; Terrace does not verify them with the vendor, insurer or licensing board. Qualification is checked when work is prepared or reassigned and again at approval and at the recorded handoff, against the vendor’s current record. Work assigned to an unqualified or unlisted vendor is prepared and shown with its issues but held: approval and handoff are refused until an owner or admin records a documented exception (a reason, kept unedited) for exactly those issues; a changed issue needs a new exception. Every operator sees holds, exceptions and assignment history on Inspections & maintenance. Only prepared, unapproved work is reassigned. Availability is recorded as text; WORK-005 adds booked appointments, and WORK-006, PORTAL-003 and every later dispatch door inherit the same qualification hold before they ship.

## WORK-005 — Book appointments and resident access

Current staff-recorded slice (ct 19 October 12:55Z): after approval of the current WORK-003 terms, a portfolio manager or maintenance coordinator (or the owner) records an arrival window inside the resident’s permitted access window, vendor availability, permission/restrictions, externally served entry-notice or documented exception reference and time, key/access instructions and the responsible staff calendar. Times carry explicit offsets matching the property’s IANA zone; skipped daylight-saving times and wrong seasonal offsets are refused, and either repeated autumn time can be selected by offset. Historical external appointments may be backfilled; all recording and confirmation timestamps remain the actual entry time in Terrace. Active proposed and confirmed windows reserve staff, vendor and unit calendars, and overlaps are refused atomically without revealing another property’s details. Both parties’ confirmations are recorded separately from external evidence, labeled “confirmed by staff, resident not contacted by Terrace”; no response stays unconfirmed. Cancellation/no-show, superseded windows, new permission, replies and reassignment are kept in history. Rescheduling makes a new appointment needing both confirmations; a vendor reassignment after cancellation/no-show makes a pending work version requiring fresh spend approval and any required exact owner decision before booking or handoff. WORK-004 qualification is rechecked at booking and confirmation. REPORT-004 measures actual preparation-to-first-dual-confirmation entry time, even if later canceled or rescheduled. PORTAL-005 now supplies separately approved arrival-window texts and cancellation notifications. A signed Y applies only to one unambiguous current permitted window with unchanged approved work and current vendor qualification; vendor confirmation remains separate. TEST-mode replies are explicitly signed fixtures. PORTAL-003 supplies vendor-portal confirmation and cancellation. New resident communication authority and actual provider delivery and reply proof remain gaps.

## WORK-006 — Dispatch and track technician progress

Exact semantics: the approved checklist (one task per line, up to 20) and scope, authorized spend, due date and approving staff contact are frozen in the work approval. An explicit staff send dispatches them through the installation mail provider to the assigned vendor or in-house technician mailbox, with the current approval’s recorded arrival window when one exists. No resident contact or key instructions are added to that packet. Provider acceptance, rejection, suppressed/unconfigured mail and uncertain outcomes are labeled as such; acceptance never claims delivery or a technician’s reply. One durable claim per approval prevents concurrent/replayed sends; after an uncertain attempt staff reconcile with the provider before arranging another handoff. Staff record sourced technician replies (accepted, en route, started, blocked, parts needed, completed) and checked approved tasks; completion requires the whole checklist, and never resolves the resident issue without WORK-008’s separate check. A missed arrival window or past property-local due date stays visibly escalated until the issue is resolved or closed; staff record whom they contacted and the next action. WORK-005 no-show/reassignment reopens exact approval, keeps the first dispatch and replies, and requires fresh approval before sending the replacement. PORTAL-003 inherits direct vendor responses; WORK-007 inherits field evidence; their absent doors are not claimed by staff-recorded progress. Dispatch snapshots and replies inherit SAFE-002 history, SAFE-004 work-evidence retention/erasure and SAFE-005 export/restore before shipping.

## WORK-007 — Capture field labor materials and completion evidence

Direct-capture semantics: a signed-in owner or portfolio manager records their own technician evidence, attributed to that operator; this introduces no vendor authentication or grant. Private device drafts keep only explicitly consented capture fields and original files, never credentials, and sync only on a person’s explicit request. Successful sync or explicit device deletion removes the draft. Changed field scope reopens exact work approval, retains old approvals/dispatch/evidence, and requires fresh authorization and handoff before manual reconciliation of the original draft. Direct reports require every current approved task, whole-minute labor (zero allowed), explicit materials/none, notes, access outcome and distinct before/after originals (photo or video); client replay cannot create another completion. They leave the resident resolution check open. Staff-recorded completion attachments currently retain whole-minute labor time (zero allowed), parts/material notes (explicit none when absent), access outcome, every approved checklist task and distinct before/after inspection originals with hashes, dates and the exact approval/scope snapshot. They are labeled sourced staff records, never direct technician submissions, and do not resolve the resident issue. A changed job version or approval requires staff to reload and review the evidence against the current work before submission. SAFE-002 history, SAFE-004 work-evidence retention/erasure and SAFE-005 backup/restore apply to these attachments.

## WORK-008 — Verify repair with the resident

Exact semantics: after the external handoff, any operator records the vendor’s completion report (completed date, what the vendor reports and where their evidence is), labeled as recorded by staff, because there is no vendor portal yet (PORTAL-003). The work then awaits the resident’s check, and staff record the resident’s answer to whether the original issue is resolved: “confirmed” counts only labeled “confirmed by staff, resident not contacted by Terrace”, “not resolved” (with what is still wrong) and “no response” carry the same “recorded by staff, resident not contacted by Terrace” wording, and Terrace never claims it asked the resident (PORTAL-005 delivers the question before it ships). No response keeps the work open and is never a confirmation. Not resolved reopens the work; a report that a resolved or closed issue recurred reopens it too. A reopened job takes a return visit: a further completion report that is a callback to the vendor, marked warranty (no charge) or billable, and then a new check. A confirmation may record satisfaction from 1 to 5; any answer may record a safety concern, which stays listed as unresolved until a later confirmation on the same job. Only an owner or admin closes open completed work without the resident’s confirmation, with a recorded reason. Reports, answers and closures are append-only, so every attempt and the final resolution stay on the job. PORTAL-003 (vendor submission), PORTAL-004 (resident status), PORTAL-005 (asking by message), PORTAL-010 (owner notice) and REPORT-004 (callback and satisfaction analytics) build on these records before they ship.

## WORK-010 — Schedule preventive and recurring maintenance

Exact semantics: a schedule belongs to a tenancy, names a directory vendor (WORK-004 qualification applies to every order), a trade, a budget per occurrence in the tenancy’s currency, an interval of 1 to 60 months from its first due date (the same day of the month, or the month’s last day when shorter; a seasonal task is an annual schedule anchored in its season’s month) and 0 to 90 lead days, and may follow an asset (WORK-002) or come from a completed inspection’s finding. A scheduler run, started by an owner or admin, creates one pending order for the budget for each occurrence whose effective due date (its deferred date, if deferred) is on or before the property-local today plus the lead days; a unique occurrence key means a replayed or concurrent run never creates a second order. An order is work an owner or admin must approve, as for any maintenance. An occurrence can be deferred, with a reason, only before it has an order. An occurrence past its effective due date whose order has not been handed off is listed as overdue. When a followed asset is replaced the schedule follows the replacement, and each occurrence keeps the asset it was ordered for. OPS-003 adds an unattended timed run, and WORK-005 vendor calendars, before they ship.

## WORK-011 — Complete mobile move-in and move-out inspections

Inspection-door semantics: new structured capture/configuration/acknowledgement/proposal doors are owner or portfolio-manager only; no new portal grant or resident authentication is introduced. Offline evidence is an explicit-consent private device draft, never an automatically submitted report; successful synchronization deletes the device draft, and a separate explicit control deletes it without sending. A changed checklist requires manual review and reconciliation, retaining the original values in the synchronized record. Resident acknowledgement is entered by staff with the resident’s words and a source, never presented as a resident signature or a Terrace contact. Entry/exit comparisons use stable room/asset keys and retain original hashes. Every proposal is held for human review; unsupported damage cannot be approved, and no inspection decision posts money, creates work or sends a message. Existing ROLE-003 independence applies to approval (including its labeled sole-operator exception).

## WORK-013 — Run make-ready and turnover boards

Exact semantics: an owner or admin opens a make-ready board for a unit (PORT-002) from a move-out date, on notice or after vacancy, when no lease occupies the unit the day after; it creates the standard tasks: move-out inspection and utility transfer first, deposit disposition and repairs after the inspection, cleaning after repairs and the listing after cleaning, each with an assignee, a duration in whole days, an optional delay and quote. A task starts the day after its last dependency ends (the first tasks the day after move-out) plus its delay; the planned ready date is the day after the last task ends, and vacancy days count from the day after move-out to it. Any operator submits a task; only an owner or admin checks it, and only after the tasks it depends on are checked; a task whose dependencies are unchecked is shown as blocked. Publishing needs every task checked and sets the unit’s available date to the later of the planned ready date and the day after the last check, in the property’s time zone. The deposit task prepares a decision only (ACCT-004 disposes the money) and the listing task prepares one (LEASE-001 publishes it); PORT-006 move-out opens the board automatically before it ships.

## WORK-014 — Control maintenance budgets inventory and chargebacks

WORK-014 implementation semantics: Annual operating and capital budgets are recorded separately for the property and optionally an in-service asset, with approved source, version and attributable history. Existing owner and scoped portfolio-manager spending authority applies. Calendar years use stored work due dates; linked payables inherit the work category, asset and due year, while unlinked payables use their recorded category, asset and invoice year. Each work reservation is the greater of its approved estimate and cumulative linked payables, including recorded tax; unlinked payables add once. Reclassification or changed work terms reopens approval; exceeding a category or asset budget requires the existing exact sourced owner decision and ROLE-003 approval. Concurrent approvals recheck their complete spend basis atomically. This budget tracking introduces no automatic approval. Stock receipts classify one complete approved unlinked property payable allocation as inventory, retaining its original beneficiary shares and recorded tax in cost; receipt never creates a second payable. Whole-part consumption transfers exact cents from inventory to repair expense within current approved work and category authority. Cumulative integer allocation per purchase beneficiary assigns the final cent on the final part. Source payables are frozen after receipt; another property, changed ownership, insufficient stock, stale work and concurrent overspend are refused. Stock evidence follows existing accounting retention, holds, eligible redaction and native archive custody. Chargebacks propose an allocation against one recorded repair invoice cost or consumed-stock beneficiary entry, never an estimate or unconsumed inventory. Existing bill preparers and independent owner or scoped portfolio-manager approvers act through ROLE-001 and ROLE-003; API credentials gain no authority. Each proposal freezes the named resident, original cost owner or insurance reference, exact cents, source date, source tax treatment and attributable exact-content authorization evidence. Source tax remains included in recovered cost; no new tax is assessed. Resident rights must cover both the source date and the current property-local date; the cost beneficiary must still own the property. Approval rechecks scope, rights, fingerprint, independence and unrecovered cents atomically, creates one receivable and offsets owner expense without another payable or a cash claim. Source invoices are frozen once proposed. Duplicate approval, concurrent excess recovery, wrong beneficiaries, stale sources and generic journal reversal are refused. The maintenance screen reconciles source cost, receivable and net owner cost; the scoped owner statement API exposes approved source allocations separately from cash. Accounting retention, subject-scoped access, eligible authorization-text redaction and native archive custody apply. No notice, dispute handling, collection, payment, insurance claim submission or legal determination occurs. WORK-015 inherits chargeback notices, disputes and collection.
