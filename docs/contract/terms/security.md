# Security privacy ownership and recovery: exact terms

The exact terms of the clauses in the “Security privacy ownership and recovery” area of [CONTRACT.md](../../../CONTRACT.md). Each clause there is the requirement; the terms below define it.

## SAFE-001 — Enforce authenticated least-privilege doors

Exact semantics: every page, API read, download, export and command reads the live Base session and operator, then ROLE-001’s capability table and portfolio scope; agents use member accounts through the same doors. Commands need Origin equal to the installation and are refused cross-site. Changes to bank references and legal entities (entity.add, account.add) and to access (operator invite, role, status, the second-factor requirement and authenticator resets) need Base’s fresh email-link proof: the sign-in link that created the session or a completed step-up link, within 10 minutes; without it the command answers 403 and writes nothing. Terrace’s own second factor is a time-based one-time password (RFC 6238: HMAC-SHA1, 30-second steps, 6 digits, one step either side) with ten single-use recovery codes stored only as hashes; a code is never accepted twice, and five wrong codes in 15 minutes lock verification for 15 minutes. Once a person confirms an authenticator, each new sign-in of theirs must pass it before any other door, Base’s own account and access doors included (pages show only the two-step page; API calls answer 403). The owner, after passing their own, may require a second factor for everyone who signs in by email link; un-enrolled staff then enroll before anything else. Staff signing in through the organization’s identity provider (OIDC) count as satisfied, under that provider’s MFA policy. Only the owner resets someone’s authenticator. Secrets are sealed with AES-GCM under a key derived from the installation’s session secret and, with recovery codes and passed sessions, are never archived, so people enroll again after a restore elsewhere. Doors not built yet inherit this before they ship: OPS-001 credentials, ACCT-018 and ROLE-003 payee bank details and notifications of change, and PORTAL-001, PORTAL-002 and PORTAL-003 bank details.

## SAFE-002 — Keep attributable immutable operating history

Exact semantics: every Terrace command that changes a lease, household, rent charge, receipt, correction, maintenance job, approval, owner record, role or setting writes one history row in the same D1 batch as the change, with the actor, the time (UTC), their role and its version at that moment (the policy they acted under; the owner’s is “owner”), the tenancy it concerns, the source they gave (a lease reference, receipt or handoff reference, or reason) and the outcome; move-in applies and undos, restores and operator activation or deactivation, which Base carries out, add a row once they succeed (Base’s own /auth doors, open only to the owner, keep Base’s records). Sensitive reads add a row with outcome “read” (owner statements, owner allocation statements, rent roll and collections reads, photo and handoff packet downloads, ACCT-011 close statement downloads) and exports one with outcome “exported” (backup, readable export, history export). The owner reads and exports all history; portfolio managers read, and accountants read and export, history about tenancies in their portfolios, and history with no tenancy only with all portfolios; others are refused (403). Filters are date (UTC, inclusive), actor, action prefix and tenancy, newest first, at most 5,000 rows a page with an older-page cursor; reading or filtering records nothing. History rows and accounting amounts are never deleted. Charges, receipts and corrections are never edited except PORT-001’s one-time AUD-to-USD relabel of an unconfirmed tenancy and SAFE-004’s fingerprint-approved redaction of resident free text to opaque record ids, which preserves actor, action, time, amounts, dates, currency and links; the database refuses anything else. MOVE-009 refuses undo of a tenancy with accounting work. Doors not built yet record their history the same way before they ship: LEASE-009 screening exports, PAY-007 and PAY-008 payment returns and reversals, PORTAL-005 and PORTAL-009 notice delivery and service, MOVE-009 import compensations (with the approved fingerprint and reason), and ACCT-018 bank instructions (ACCT-011 closes, reopenings and corrections already do).

## SAFE-004 — Apply reviewed retention holds and erasure

Exact semantics (ct 19 October 12:55Z): the reviewed default periods are lease records 7 calendar years after lease end, accounting identity 7 years after its fiscal year (the calendar year in today’s books), screening 2 years after decision or 5 for adverse action, and communications 3 years after communication. The anniversary is the last retained day, inclusive in the property time zone, with 29 February clamped to 28 February; a PORT-008 pack may only lengthen a default before it ships. Today’s lease evidence includes household, inspection originals/photos, work approvals and access records; accounting includes charges, receipts, reductions, fee source evidence and month closes. The installation owner records a resident access or erasure request with its external reference (no resident contact by Terrace), downloads that tenancy’s primary-resident access file without other household or owner contacts, and reviews retained classes, dates and reasons. Only the owner places or releases a class or all-record legal hold, with a reason. Erasure requires that request’s current review fingerprint and exact resident email; changed holds or terms require a new review. A lease period or lease/all hold prevents deletion of lease evidence. Once eligible, its originals, photos, work and import copies/search provenance are removed atomically with the restore fence; accounting identity stays while its period or hold applies. After both expire or are released, the tenancy’s personal fields and financial free-text references, fee labels, close references and resident-linked audit source text become an opaque alias/record id; amounts, currencies, dates, financial links, close rows and attributable audit events are kept, and an ordinary update still cannot alter them. The erased tenancy disappears from operational reads; retained accounting remains in exports. The hashed resident-email suppression prevents reimport, and an older archive is refused when the later erasure journal is supplied. No screening records, delivered resident communications, external provider artifacts or external search index exist today: LEASE-009, PORT-007, PORTAL-005, PORTAL-006 and OPS-004 inherit class retention, scoped access/erasure, holds, removal from every stored copy/search/media/provider and the same restore fence before adding them. Each future record kind or door, including ACCT-007 bills, inherits SAFE-004 before shipping.

## SAFE-005 — Leave with complete usable operating data

Exact semantics: the owner downloads two exports. The backup (.rer, Base’s documented portability format) holds every table except platform bookkeeping, Base sign-in records and the named secrets (the capability table, authenticators, recovery codes, passed sessions and failed attempts); it restores only into an empty installation, keeping identifiers and references, and never brings sessions or operator accounts across. The readable export (.zip) is built from that same archive: one CSV per table with a header row (money in whole cents beside its currency, ISO dates, UTC timestamps), every stored file as its original bytes under files/ with its SHA-256 in the CSV cell that held it, a manifest of tables, row counts, columns, file checksums and what was left out, and a README listing what to reconnect after a restore: mail delivery, people and their roles, authenticators, any identity provider, and that Terrace moves no money so a restore repeats no payment. Every record table a later clause adds is archived and exported the same way unless it is a named secret; PORT-007 adds its stored documents and media as original files, PORTAL-005 and PORTAL-006 their communications, OPS-006 and LEASE-012 their signed files, and OPS-004 each connected provider to the reconnect list, before they ship.

## SAFE-007 — Run an owned installation with honest diagnostics

Exact current-capability semantics (20 October 2026): upgrades are reviewed Git merges in a separate installation restored from the owner’s backup, preserving committed owner source changes, private configuration and existing data; applied migrations are never reset or rewritten. Health and local diagnostics describe runtime identity and missing inputs, never provider verification. Current provider setup is installation email through Resend and owned Cloudflare deployment; LEASE-002/003/004/006/008/011, PAY-002/003/004/007, ACCT-005/008/010/014/015/017/018, PORTAL-005/009, WORK-012 and OPS-002/004/005/006/007 inherit documented setup, redacted operational errors and no unsolicited telemetry before their provider capabilities ship.
