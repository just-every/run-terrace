# Trust accounting and owner finance: exact terms

The exact terms of the clauses in the “Trust accounting and owner finance” area of [CONTRACT.md](../../../CONTRACT.md). Each clause there is the requirement; the terms below define it.

## ACCT-001 — Maintain a balanced property ledger

Exact accounting semantics: [the property-ledger guide](docs/property-ledger.md) states the implemented chart, exact-cent source entries, immutable reversals, per-owner/property/fund cash fence, dated-owner allocation, legacy opaque beneficiary keys, USD boundary, role/scope, month-close snapshots and retention. This records external facts and never moves funds. A reversing receipt retains the original received-date owner allocation and agreement; its fee-run correction reverses the recorded fee and tax, including prior overrides, with exact inverse cent rounding. ACCT-002/003/004/008/010/012/013/018 and PAY-002/003/004/007/008/009/012/013 inherit balanced source entries, reversal tracing and beneficiary/fund isolation before their money actions ship.

## ACCT-002 — Separate operating trust and deposit accounts

Exact custody semantics: [the trust-account guide](docs/trust-accounts.md) describes USD bank references, legal-entity/property authorizations, immutable designated purposes, per-bank/property/beneficiary cash, journal-to-custody reconciliation, historical unassigned exceptions and reserve-checked same-beneficiary transfers. This records external bank facts, never initiates money movement or claims bank-statement verification. ACCT-003/004/008/010/017/018 and PAY-002/003/007/008/009/012/013 inherit authorized account/fund/beneficiary isolation and source tracing before their money actions ship; ACCT-017/018 must enforce exactly one paying bank account per check run/file/provider batch. The authorization rule is owner-only bank configuration and historical assignment with fresh email-link proof; scoped bookkeeper/portfolio-manager preparation and external recording; portfolio-manager/owner approval under ROLE-003, with fresh proof for approval/recording and live authority/reserve rechecks.

## ACCT-006 — Match statements and perform three-way reconciliation

ACCT-017 and ACCT-018 inherit check-register and transmitted-file source matching before their doors ship.

## ACCT-007 — Capture and approve vendor bills

Exact semantics (ct 19 October 13:49Z): the owner, portfolio managers and bookkeepers capture an original PDF, JPEG, PNG or .eml email up to 5 MB privately and enter its supplier from the vendor directory, invoice number/date, USD line descriptions, expense codes, pre-tax amounts and tax in exact cents; Terrace does not extract or validate the invoice with a provider. Attachments download only for those roles and accountants reaching every current and historical allocated property; out-of-scope records are 404, other roles 403. Supplier plus case-folded invoice number (including prior revisions), and the exact bytes of an original, prevent duplicates. Property allocations including tax must equal the bill total. Approval needs dated owner shares and a management agreement for every property; a linked work order must belong to that property, have the same supplier and currency and approved terms. Per-job limits, annual budgets, approved work amounts and cumulative work-linked invoices are checked; the budget uses the larger of each reserved approved estimate or its approved actual invoices, plus unlinked payables, rather than counting linked work twice. Missing source/authority is held. A manager or owner records the owner’s exact source-backed decision for an over-limit, over-budget or over-estimate bill, labeled recorded by staff for the owner, then a different owner or portfolio manager approves its exact revision under ROLE-003 (sole operator approval is explicitly labeled). Approval records an unpaid payable, splits each property’s cost using PORT-003 shares effective on the invoice date, and records the dated agreement’s markup and tax on markup, half up after the owner split. The owner allocation statement shows these expenses, fees and agreement references and subtracts them from available USD cash with the reserve share in force at period end (property ids identify reserves, even when names repeat). Changing terms, payee or attachments makes a pending revision and reverses prior markup with new lines; earlier snapshots stay and the changed obligation needs fresh approval. Monthly recurring templates carry payee, amount/lines, allocations, first/last bill date and day of month; days 29–31 clamp to the month’s last day, and a run is allowed only when that date has arrived at every allocated property. Exact template approval and each period bill’s separate approval are required; edits reopen template approval without changing bills already generated, and a unique template/month key makes replay safe. Closed months refuse new or changed bills and markup. ACCT-008 and ACCT-018 carry payment execution; no money moves here. SAFE-004 applies: work-linked subject references survive work deletion, accounting periods and either resident’s hold retain shared originals (the other subject’s hold is explained without identity or reason); once eligible, originals and personal revision/decision/audit text are erased/redacted under the erasure gate while amounts, dates, allocations and original fingerprints stay. A minimal erased-file hash suppresses reupload and the archive journal blocks restoration of older copies. Resident access lists their shared accounting source ids without another party’s invoice text or amounts. SAFE-005 archives private originals, revisions and owner expense rows; ACCT-012 expands full owner statements and WORK-009 adds invoice extraction/matching before they ship.

## ACCT-009 — Calculate management fees and owner reserves

Exact semantics: fee terms belong to PORT-003’s dated management agreement, the one in force on each fee’s day: the management fee percent of collected rent, a flat leasing fee per new lease, a flat renewal fee, a monthly minimum management fee, a tax rate on fees and a markup percent for vendor bills, all in USD; the reserve floor is the agreement’s reserve requirement. A property’s fee run for a month (this month or earlier, property-local; the owner, portfolio managers and bookkeepers in its portfolio) records one line per owner and source: the management fee on each owner’s share of every confirmed-USD receipt received that month (receipts on ACCT-016 tax charges excepted) (share split and fee rounding as PORT-003: floor per share, remainder cents to the largest share, fee half up to the cent; receipts in another or an unconfirmed currency are listed as skipped), the leasing fee on each lease starting that month on one of its units (a unit transfer is not a new lease), the renewal fee on each renewal approved that month (its property-local approval day), and, once the month has ended, the top-up that brings that month’s management fees to the monthly minimum; flat fees are split by the shares in force on their day. Tax on each line is the agreement’s rate of the fee, half up to the cent, as recorded; Terrace files and remits no tax. Each line names its source (receipt reference, lease, renewal or minimum), base, rate, agreement and run. A rerun posts only the difference between what the records now call for and what is recorded, so nothing is charged twice, and two runs at once cannot both post; fee lines are never edited or deleted, and a run or override for a month closed under ACCT-011 is refused until it is reopened. Only the owner overrides a fee (ROLE-001 money authority): the override is a new line setting that owner’s fee for that source to the stated amount with a reason, and later runs leave that source alone. The PORT-003 owner allocation statement lists each owner’s recorded fee lines with their agreement reference and the owner’s USD cash available: their share of receipts less recorded fees, tax on fees and their reserve floor share in force at the period end; ACCT-010 distributions never exceed it before they ship, ACCT-007 vendor bills now apply the agreement markup and carry approved expenses into it; ACCT-012 full owner statements inherit these lines before shipping.

## ACCT-011 — Close periods without rewriting history

Exact semantics: a period is a calendar month of the recorded rent ledger, installation-wide; a posting belongs to the month of its date (a charge’s due date, a receipt’s received date, a charge reduction’s property-local day of recording). Only a month that has ended in the office time zone (PORT-001) can be signed off or closed, and only by the owner or an all-portfolio bookkeeper (ROLE-001). The month-end checklist has three sign-offs, each kept with who signed, when and an optional note: every receipt for the month is recorded; recorded receipts agree with the bank deposits, labeled as checked by staff because Terrace has not matched a bank statement (ACCT-006 adds statement matching and makes this item a matched reconciliation before it ships); and open receivables and charge reductions are reviewed. The exception queue lists the month’s charges still unpaid and its pending close corrections; a month closes only with all three sign-offs and no pending correction. A close is a numbered version that keeps a statement of every posting dated in the month, totaled per currency (charges, receipts, reductions and net change, never converted); while a month has a close that is not superseded, the database refuses any charge due or receipt received in it, and any ACCT-009 fee line or override for it (409 `period_closed`). The owner or a bookkeeper reopens a closed month with a reason: that version is marked superseded with the reason, who reopened it and when, and is never edited or deleted; new sign-offs are needed and the next close is the next version, shown beside the earlier ones with the entries added and removed since the version before (earlier entries stay as recorded). A close correction is a missed charge or a missed receipt in an ended month, prepared by an accountant, bookkeeper or portfolio manager with a reason; it posts nothing until another bookkeeper, portfolio manager or the owner approves the exact prepared entry (ROLE-003: the preparer never approves, except “self-approved: no second person”), and approval is refused while its month is closed. A month’s statement of record is its close version: today’s ledger, owner and allocation statements are generated from the live ledger, and ACCT-012 published owner statements mark those covering a reopened month superseded before they ship; ACCT-001 double-entry postings and ACCT-007 vendor bills are refused in closed months the same way before they ship. Sign-offs, closes, reopenings and corrections are history rows (SAFE-002), and close statement downloads are recorded reads.

## ACCT-016 — Account for utility recoveries and ancillary income

Exact semantics: a utility bill (water, sewer, trash, gas or electric) is recorded per property with its vendor, source invoice reference, service period, resident due date, amount, recoverable share (default 100%, half up to the cent), formula and optional per-lease cap, by the owner, a portfolio manager or a bookkeeper. Its recoverable amount is split over everyone who occupied the property’s units in the service period, inclusive of both days: occupant-days (the primary tenant plus co-tenants and occupants, each for the days they were on the lease; guarantors and contacts do not count), bedroom-days (a studio counts as one bedroom), unit-days, or submeter reading × days; under the unit-weighted formulas a unit’s vacant days take their share. Each party’s share is the floor of its part, with remainder cents to the largest basis. A lease is charged its share only when a recorded clause of that lease authorizes recovery of that utility, and at most the cap; vacancy, unauthorized leases and capped excess stay unrecovered (the owner’s cost). The allocation is reviewed: another person with approval authority approves the exact allocation shown (ROLE-003 independence; a changed allocation must be reviewed again), and approval posts one charge per charged lease, referenced by invoice and unit and describing the invoice, period, formula and basis, so the posted charges total the approved recoverable amount. Parking, storage and other permitted charges post one-off to a lease, and parking and utility charges also recur through PAY-001 terms. A tax rule names a property, a charge kind (utility, parking, storage or other), a rate and its start; it applies only once an accountant or the owner has reviewed it, and the reviewed rule in force on a charge’s due date adds a separate tax charge, half up to the cent, naming the rule. Terrace computes tax as configured and files or remits nothing. Every such charge is in the resident’s ledger; receipts on recoveries and ancillary charges are owner income in PORT-003 allocation and ACCT-009 fees, and receipts on tax charges never are. ACCT-007 records the utility bill as the owner’s payable and ACCT-012 owner statements show the bill beside its recoveries, and PORT-007 attaches the source invoice document, before they ship.

## ACCT-018 — Originate trust ACH payments through NACHA files

**Bank file rail (default):** the manager’s own bank is the originating bank. For each approved batch Terrace generates one NACHA-format file per paying operating, trust or deposit bank account, never mixing accounts: PPD entries for individual payees, CCD entries with a remittance addenda record for business payees; immediate destination and origin, company ID, company entry description and file ID modifier from the bank’s ACH settings; a balanced offset entry to the paying account or an unbalanced file, per the bank setting; 94-character records blocked to multiples of ten; batch and file control records with entry and addenda counts, entry hash and debit and credit totals; and an effective entry date from the approved pay date and the bank’s cutoff. File, batch and trace numbers derive from the approved batch, so regenerating returns the same file and never a second batch. The person who uploads the file to the bank portal, or the configured bank SFTP transmission, records the bank’s upload or confirmation reference; entries stay `transmitted`, not `settled`, until the bank feed shows them. **Provider API rail (optional):** Modern Treasury payment orders on the manager’s connected bank account for same-day or next-day ACH, under the same one-account-per-batch, idempotency and approval rules, with provider webhooks for sent, returned and reversed states. **Payees:** a new or changed payee bank account is verified by a zero-dollar prenote or provider micro-deposits (or Plaid Auth when the payee links an account) before its first live entry, and a bank-detail change follows ROLE-003 independent verification. **Returns and notifications of change:** Terrace imports the bank’s ACH return and notification-of-change (NOC) files, or the provider’s webhooks. A return (R01, R02, R03, R04 and the other R codes) reverses the entry, reopens the payable, distribution or refund for new approval and suspends the payee method. A NOC (C01 to C07) proposes the corrected account, routing or account-type detail, which is held for ROLE-003 independent verification before use, and its correction deadline is tracked. Every payee gets a remittance notice by email and portal listing the bills, distribution statement or refund the entry pays. Settled entries clear against the bank feed (ACCT-005) and three-way reconciliation (ACCT-006) by trace number, batch total or entry amount.
