# Leasing acquisition and execution: exact terms

The exact terms of the clauses in the “Leasing acquisition and execution” area of [CONTRACT.md](../../../CONTRACT.md). Each clause there is the requirement; the terms below define it.

## LEASE-005 — Work prospect records and enquiries

Exact semantics (19 October 2026): the owner, portfolio managers and leasing staff record each lead on a property in their portfolios with its source (own site listing, marketplace listing, email, phone, walk-in, referral) and the listing, message or call reference; a public enquiry form is LEASE-001’s door and creates the same prospect with source “own site listing”. A lead whose email (case-insensitive) or phone (digits, a leading US 1 dropped) matches an open, unmerged prospect in the same portfolio is held as a possible duplicate and cannot be worked until a person merges it (both histories and sources kept, the first contact’s source and reference fixed, per-channel consent the most restrictive of the two) or keeps it separate; nothing merges by itself. Household preferences are bedrooms, occupants, move-in date, monthly budget in exact cents, pets and notes; consent is granted, refused or unknown per channel (email, phone, text) with its time and source, and a response on a refused channel is refused. Staff email a prospect from the record through the installation’s mail provider only while email consent is granted (an opt-out records it refused and stops sending): the written message is kept in the history before sending and the provider’s own outcome beside it (accepted by the provider with delivery not confirmed, rejected, rate-limited, uncertain, unreachable, suppressed in development or demo, or no provider configured), replies going to the sender’s own email; phone and text responses are logged as “recorded by staff; Terrace did not contact the prospect” until PORTAL-005 (ct ruling 19:01Z). One assigned person (an active owner, portfolio manager or leasing staff member in that portfolio). Stages move forward only: enquiry, showing scheduled, shown, applied, offer, leased; or lost, with a reason (chose another home, price, unit no longer available, did not qualify, stopped responding, other with a note). Showings and applications are staff-recorded stages with a date and reference until LEASE-006 and LEASE-007 carry them. Members read prospects; bookkeepers, accountants and maintenance coordinators get 403; another portfolio’s prospect is 404; prospects hold no bank data. Prospect records are kept 3 years after their last activity (SAFE-004 communications) and the owner erases one on request, history keeping only an opaque alias.

## LEASE-012 — Run renewal and retention campaigns

Exact semantics (20 October 2026, ct ruling): anyone holding `renewal.prepare` schedules an inclusive window of at most 367 days of lease end dates (optionally one property) within their portfolios, creating one renewal per current, not-ended tenancy and lease end, never twice; a scheduled lease end can no longer take TER-003’s direct recorded renewal, which remains labelled recorded terms for leases without campaign work. An offer names the proposed end and rent per the tenancy’s cadence, a reply deadline from today through the current lease end, a dated market rent (an assumption, with its source), expected vacant days (0–365) and make-ready and leasing costs, each with its source; its recipients are frozen as the household’s current PORT-004 notice recipients who have an email address. The comparison, frozen with the offer, is the change from current rent in cents and in basis points of current rent, the difference from the market rent in cents and basis points of the market rent (both rounded half away from zero to 0.01%), and turnover cost = vacant days × current rent × periods per year (12, 26 or 52) ÷ 365, rounded half up once to the cent, plus make-ready and leasing costs. An owner or portfolio manager approves the exact offer under `proposal.approve` with ROLE-003 independence (sole-operator label); approval changes no lease. Owner, portfolio manager or leasing staff send an approved offer explicitly through the installation mail provider, with reply-to the sending staff mailbox; each recipient’s attempt is recorded before sending and the provider’s answer after it; “accepted” means the provider accepted the email, never delivery; an accepted or uncertain attempt is never sent again; demo and development mail are suppressed and labelled not sent. Replies (accepted, declined, counteroffer, unclear) and outcomes (no response, only after the deadline; nonrenewal; month-to-month) are staff-recorded with their date and source, never inbox-synced; a counteroffer reopens the offer. Each state names its next action, and every renewal stays in the follow-up queue until its renewed terms activate; nonrenewal and month-to-month send no legal notice and convert nothing automatically. After an accepted reply, an owner or portfolio manager records the signed renewal as a PORT-005 lease version effective the day after the current end, through the offer’s end, whose rent equals the accepted offer; it is labelled “signature recorded by staff, not verified by an e-signature provider”. Another person approves its PAY-001 preview; that approval, once only, retains the prior term in history, extends the tenancy and activates the versioned schedule atomically, and the renewal fee (ACCT-009) is earned on that day. LEASE-011 inherits electronic signature of renewals by name before it ships.
