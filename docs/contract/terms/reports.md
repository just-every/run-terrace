# Reports and portfolio performance: exact terms

The exact terms of the clauses in the “Reports and portfolio performance” area of [CONTRACT.md](../../../CONTRACT.md). Each clause there is the requirement; the terms below define it.

## REPORT-001 — Explain the complete rent roll

Exact semantics: the rent roll lists every unit (PORT-002) and every lease without a unit record that is in effect, as of the chosen date in the property’s calendar. A unit is occupied when a lease covers that day (its start to its move-out day or end date), otherwise vacant, naming any future lease. Contract rent on the day is the rent of the lease period covering it: a renewal’s earlier terms until that period ends, then the renewed rent (TER-003). Market rent is the unit’s recorded market rent. Recurring charges and concessions are the PAY-001 terms in effect that day, linked by term to the lease they drill to. Totals count units, occupied and vacant units, and sum contract and market rent per currency, never across currencies. Filters by property and occupancy apply to the page and to the CSV, XLSX and PDF downloads alike. The collection view for a month takes the charges due in that month: billed is their amount, collected is the recorded external receipts against them, a lease is paid in full when nothing remains after receipts and reductions, and its payment state is paid by the recorded method (ACH, card, cash, check or other; “method not recorded” for receipts entered before this rule), partial or unpaid. The method is what a manager records; Terrace has not processed or verified it. Pending funds are reported as none because Terrace holds none; PAY-002, PAY-003 and PAY-007 add processor receipts and pending funds, and ACCT-003 adds deposit obligations to the roll (shown as not recorded until then), before they ship.

## REPORT-002 — Report delinquency and collection performance

Exact semantics (20 October 2026, ct ruling): done for current recorded capabilities; PAY-008 inherits returned/reversed ACH and pending/attempted-collection outcomes, PAY-011 inherits active/broken plans, notices and resolved outcomes, and PAY-013 inherits tenant/HAP separation and the corresponding acceptance cases before shipping. The as-of date is an inclusive property-local day no later than today. Each due charge’s outstanding is exact charge cents minus external receipts received through that day minus reductions on their explicit approved posting day, or property-local recorded day when absent; future-due charges and their allocations are excluded. This reconstructs effective dates from records known now, not what staff knew then. Due today is current; earlier balances age by calendar days into 1–30, 31–60, 61–90 and over 90. Paid lines remain drillable; one household shares one balance, never multiplied by co-tenants. USD, AUD and currency-unconfirmed balances remain separate. Property/household filters apply existing reports.read portfolio scope. Owner filters select the ownership set effective on asOf; a matching owner selects the full household balance once, not a percentage allocation, and missing ownership remains unknown. Staff see dated charge, receipt, reduction and ownership sources and export the same filtered CSV without screening data. External receipts are not settled-bank proof; PAY-005 manually recorded pending funds are dated separately and never deducted from receivables. Linked negative receipts include only source-limited recorded refunds/reversals. Processor pending payments, returned ACH, plans, broken promises, delivered notices, collection outcomes and HAP splits remain not recorded, never inferred zero or resolved. Owner/portfolio-manager/bookkeeper in the household’s portfolio assigns an active owner/portfolio-manager/bookkeeper as operational follow-up owner, without send or collect authority; absent or revoked/out-of-scope/restored assignees read “unassigned — assign follow-up owner”. Assignment changes are versioned and audited with commit-time live actor/target scope checks. With an eligible assignee and a latest approved arrears draft, the next action is “review and send externally; Terrace has not sent it”; otherwise prepare and review a proposal. Assignment history and approved drafts are dated through asOf, while eligibility is checked live. SAFE-004 treats assignment references/events as accounting follow-up evidence: seven-year fiscal retention from their property-local recorded day and accounting holds; personal redaction deletes those references/events while preserving financial amounts under the opaque alias. Resident access includes the subject’s assignment dates/references without staff identity. SAFE-005 archives/exports/restores the history with opaque source staff IDs, which grant no destination authority; import undo is held after an assignment as later native work.

## REPORT-003 — Measure vacancy leasing and renewal performance

Exact semantics (20 October 2026, ct ruling): current capabilities conform now; LEASE-001, LEASE-002 and LEASE-003 add actual listing publication/freshness before they ship, and LEASE-012 adds campaign, reply and executed-renewal outcomes before it ships. The report uses inclusive property-local windows of at most 732 days ending no later than each selected property’s today, and inventory begins on its recorded creation day. Occupancy is occupied rentable unit-days divided by rentable unit-days; leases cover start through move-out or end inclusive. Current rentable status is a snapshot, not reconstructed historical inventory. Vacancy spells retain their recorded beginning and are clipped to the window; days to lease is the nonnegative local-day difference from vacancy start to the next tenancy’s recorded creation, never move-in or signature evidence. Future move-ins and current make-ready ready dates are separately labeled schedules/forecasts; a published available date is a recorded actual. Listing status says “listing not recorded (LEASE-001); freshness unknown”, never zero stale listings. Economic vacancy is (estimated potential rent minus recorded external rent receipts received in the window) divided by estimated potential rent, and may be negative. Potential uses the current USD monthly market-rent snapshot, explicitly an assumption taken at report time, divided by each calendar month’s actual days and summed as exact rational cents, rounded half up once across the unit or report. Missing-market units are excluded from both potential and receipts. Only confirmed USD receipts on term-classified rent charges count; unlinked manual receipts, unconfirmed/other currencies and missing market rents are separate coverage gaps, never inferred zero or converted. A receipt is not bank settlement. First-contact lead cohorts exclude merged/held duplicate rows, keep reviewed-separate contacts, and count only dated staff stage events effective and recorded by the window end; skipped stages are never inferred. Source conversion is leased divided by leads; showing/application drop-off is the number with a shown/applied event lacking an applied/leased event, divided by the corresponding actual stage count. Renewal retention is approved recorded renewals divided by original lease-term expiries in the window, preserving prior ends through renewal history and separating pending, rejected and nonrenewed cases; a LEASE-012 campaign offer counts as renewed only once its signed renewal activated by the window end, its scheduling, send results, replies and outcomes are dated sources, and a declined, nonrenewal, no-response or month-to-month outcome counts as nonrenewed. It never claims delivery or electronic signatures; a signature is reported as staff-recorded. Zero denominators are not measurable. Existing reports.read holders read scoped aggregates and unit/lease/receipt sources; only those who also hold prospect.read receive prospect drill IDs, events and references.

## REPORT-004 — Report maintenance service and cost

Exact semantics: the report covers maintenance jobs on tenancies in the reader’s portfolios (ROLE-001: owner, portfolio managers, maintenance coordinators, bookkeepers and accountants), filtered by the property-local date the job was prepared (inclusive), property and status (all, unresolved, or one status). Each job shows its status (waiting for approval, approved, handed off, completion reported and not confirmed, reopened, resolved with the resident’s confirmation, or closed without it), request age in whole days from preparation to the office’s today while open, response time in hours from preparation to the first approval, handoff time to the recorded external handoff, and resolution time to the resident’s confirmation only while that confirmation is the job’s current outcome; a vendor’s completion report, a reopened job or a closure without confirmation has no resolution time. Repeat repairs are jobs with a warranty or billable return visit (WORK-008). Vendor performance counts each vendor’s jobs, resolved, reopened, return visits and average resolution hours. Asset cost sums the estimates of each asset’s jobs whose current terms are approved or handed off; budget variance compares each owner yearly budget (WORK-003) with the approved and handed-off estimates due that year, as WORK-003 counts them. Costs are labeled approved estimates, not invoices (WORK-009 carries invoiced cost). Missing timestamps are listed per job (no approval, no handoff, no completion report), and booking time is preparation to the first appointment confirmed by both parties externally and recorded by staff (WORK-005), or explicitly missing; open emergencies remain not recorded until WORK-001 (emergency triage), which inherits this report before it ships. The filtered list downloads as CSV.

## REPORT-005 — Build governed custom reports and fields

Exact semantics: fields and definitions are portfolio-scoped on units, properties or owners, using existing ROLE-001 rights; chosen read roles restrict those rights. Fields support text, finite decimal number, boolean, date and select values, with required/range/choice validation and dated source/version history. Numeric formulas use add/subtract/multiply/divide with IEEE-754 arithmetic and no monetary rounding; missing operands yield null, numeric aggregates ignore null and division by zero is refused. Reports group selected columns, filter typed source fields and retain schema pins; a changed source schema requires explicit definition revision (409). Templates merge custom field keys into plain text. Generated documents freeze text and template/schema/value/source versions; later edits create new versions, while downloads recheck current and historical field access.

## REPORT-007 — Expose cash and vacancy trade-offs

Exact semantics: on a property-linked tenancy the owner or a staff role holding the existing reports.read capability compares two read-only plans: repair before listing and immediate marketing with a concession. Both require an explicit inclusive property-local horizon, dated source reference and confidence/uncertainty note, plus source-backed rent per tenancy cadence, optional assumed renewal date and new rent, make-ready days, letting days, total concession, opening operating cash, deposits already held/new receipts/refunds, capital or additional costs, planned distributions and collection percent. Zero is entered explicitly; missing cash or deposit history is never invented. The comparison sources the tenancy’s recorded rent/cadence/version, the current approved unpaid vendor-bill allocation for that property once per current bill revision, and dated management agreements; it names each invoice/revision and agreement. The reserve is the requirement in force at horizon end, never a claim that cash is held; the earlier dated reserve schedule is retained in the result. Confirmed USD is required because reserves and payables are USD; historical amounts are never relabeled or converted. Without the property or an agreement effective by horizon start the report shows a source gap. Repairs run make-ready then letting, so vacant days are their sum; immediate marketing runs them concurrently, so vacant days are their maximum. The ready date is the horizon start plus vacant days and is the first assumed rent day; vacancy within the horizon is capped at its inclusive day count. Daily rent uses each calendar month’s actual days for monthly cadence, seven for weekly and fourteen for fortnightly; an assumed renewal changes rent from its stated day, and forecasts explicitly continue rent over the horizon as an assumption rather than extending the actual lease. Exact rational daily rent is summed and rounded half up once to cents; expected collected rent applies the user’s 0–100% estimate to the exact gross before that rounding. The full concession, current payables once (regardless of their invoice dates), capital/additional costs and final reserve floor reduce opening operating cash plus collected rent; distributions reduce the result and a negative result shows a potential shortfall, never payment authority. A current payable is not repeated in capital assumptions. Deposits are separately shown as opening held plus new expected less refunds and never become operating cash or owner distributions; collection percent does not discount deposits. Other expected fees, taxes or operating costs need an explicit additional-cost assumption; no bank balance, arrears or reconciliation is inferred. Changing assumptions recalculates cash and ready dates without storing scenarios, changing approvals, posting ledger entries or approving rent. Existing role and portfolio read scope apply on the page and API. LEASE-012, PAY-013 and ACCT-003/010/012 inherit sourcing their renewal, payer, deposit, distribution and statement actuals into this comparison before those records ship; until then all those forecast categories remain explicit user assumptions.
