# Portfolio, leases and operating rights: exact terms

The exact terms of the clauses in the “Portfolio, leases and operating rights” area of [CONTRACT.md](../../../CONTRACT.md). Each clause there is the requirement; the terms below define it.

## PORT-001 — Run a US portfolio in USD

Exact semantics: every money record (tenancy rent, charge, receipt, charge reduction, renewal history and maintenance estimate) stores its ISO 4217 currency. A new tenancy is USD; an imported historical tenancy may be AUD and stays AUD. Records entered before this rule were labeled A$ and then $ without a stored currency, so their currency is unconfirmed: a tenancy that existed when Terrace began storing currencies (migration `0018_us_portfolio.sql` marked it AUD) is “currency unconfirmed” unless it was imported with a property time zone (only a PORT-001 tenancy CSV sets one, with an explicit currency), and a transfer of an unconfirmed lease is unconfirmed too. Its money, and every charge, receipt, reduction, renewal, estimate, term, schedule and asset of it, is shown as the amount followed by “(currency unconfirmed)”, never as A$ or $, and every total (the Tenancies home, the rent roll and the collection view) lists unconfirmed money as its own line, never summed with USD or AUD. The installation owner, and only the owner, confirms once per installation on Data & access that those records were AUD or USD; the confirmation is recorded with who, when and how many tenancies, audited, and refused a second time. Confirming AUD marks them confirmed AUD; confirming USD relabels them and their records USD. Either way no amount changes and no conversion is claimed. Apart from that one confirmation, a record’s currency never changes, a charge, receipt or reduction carries its tenancy’s, amounts are never converted and totals are kept per currency. Amounts are integer cents: an entry with more than two decimal places is refused, never rounded. Each tenancy stores its property’s IANA time zone: a US zone when created or changed (defaulting to the office zone), any IANA zone for an imported historical tenancy, and UTC for records entered before this rule until a manager sets one. Due, received, lease and follow-up dates are property-local calendar days; “today”, overdue balances and the future-receipt check use the property-local day; a recorded instant counts on the property-local day it falls on; and a statement period runs from the start of its first day to the start of the day after its last day, each shown with that day’s UTC offset. The company’s office time zone (America/New_York until the company sets one) dates the Tenancies home. An EIN is a nine-digit reference checked for format only, never verified with the IRS; an operating account is a recorded USD reference (legal entity, name, bank and last four digits) with no full account or routing number, and Terrace moves no money from it. A property (PORT-002) carries its zone and a tenancy placed on one of its units takes it; a tenancy without a unit record carries its own. PAY-001 and every later clause that adds a money record, schedule or date-bearing record inherit the stored-currency and property-local rules before they ship.

## PORT-002 — Separate properties buildings and units

Exact semantics: a portfolio holds properties; a property (a building of kind single-family, duplex, multifamily, townhouse or condo) has an address and a US time zone; a unit has an identifier unique within its property, bedrooms, bathrooms in halves, amenities and a rentable flag. A tenancy placed on a unit takes the unit’s address and its property’s time zone; a tenancy recorded without a unit keeps its own address. A unit holds at most one lease on any day (co-tenants share one lease, PORT-004), and a unit that is not rentable takes no new lease. A lease occupies its unit from its start to its move-out day when ended early, otherwise to its end date; ending early keeps the record and the original end date. A transfer ends the lease the day before the move-in and starts a linked lease on the other unit with the same parties, rent and end date unless changed. Occupancy is read as of the property-local today: occupied, vacant with a future lease, or vacant. MOVE-001 importers, REPORT-001, ROLE-001 property scoping and PORT-006 move-in and move-out inherit units before they ship.

## PORT-003 — Manage owners and management agreements

Exact semantics: an owner is an individual or entity with a legal name, email and mailing address, kept apart from a tenancy’s recorded owner contact, which is never rewritten. W-9 status is not requested, requested on a date, or received on a date with where the signed form is kept, recorded by staff; Terrace stores no taxpayer identification number and never claims TIN or IRS validation. Each property has dated share sets in basis points that total exactly 100%; the set in force on a day is the one with the latest effective date on or before it, so a transfer recorded from a date ends the seller’s share the day before and starts the buyer’s that day, and a new set must start after the latest one (history is never edited). A management agreement per property records its start, the fee as a percent of collected rent, the reserve requirement in USD and its document reference; the agreement in force on a day is the latest started on or before it. The reserve requirement is an amount owed to reserves, not cash held (ACCT-001 and ACCT-010 carry held reserves). Each recorded receipt on a tenancy on the property (except receipts on ACCT-016 tax charges, which are never owner income) is allocated by the share set in force on its received date: each owner gets the floor of the exact cents times their share, and any remainder cents go to the largest share (ties to the owner listed first); the fee is the agreement percent of each allocated amount, rounded half up to the cent. Each owner’s reserve share is the requirement split the same way, per stretch of the period with one share set and one agreement in force. An owner allocation statement for an owner and an inclusive period lists each allocated receipt with its share, fee and net, totals per currency, and the reserve shares, on each side of any transfer date. Owner records, W-9 status, share sets and agreements are installation-wide records kept by the owner and all-portfolio portfolio managers (ROLE-001); bookkeepers, accountants and managers download allocation statements for properties in their portfolios, and leasing staff, maintenance coordinators and members see owner names and shares only. Expenses are allocated the same way when vendor bills ship (ACCT-007) and full owner statements carry them (ACCT-012).

## PORT-004 — Keep resident households and occupants

Exact semantics: a lease’s tenant is its primary resident; its household adds co-tenants, guarantors, occupants, emergency contacts and authorized contacts, each with contact details, a contact preference (email, phone, mail or none) and the days they joined and left. The primary resident, co-tenants and guarantors are liable for that lease’s balance; occupants and contacts are not. The recipients of lease notices and statements are the current primary resident, co-tenants, guarantors and authorized contacts whose preference is not none; the current primary resident, co-tenants, guarantors and authorized contacts hold lease document rights whatever their preference; emergency contacts and occupants have neither. Current means joined on or before the property-local today and whose leaving day, if any, is not before property-local today. A person on two leases has a separate household row on each, so liabilities and rights are per lease. Moving a member ends their membership the day before the move and adds them to the other lease from that day; each lease keeps its balance. Erasing a tenancy erases its household. Terrace lists recipients and sends nothing: PORTAL-005 and PORTAL-009 deliver to this list, and PORTAL-001 portal access and PORT-007 documents enforce these rights, before they ship.

Revised 22 October 2026: PORT-004 primary residents use the same property-local joined/left predicate as added household members. Before joining, they have neither notice-recipient nor document rights; both dates are inclusive.

## PORT-005 — Version complete lease obligations

Exact semantics: an owner or portfolio manager records an original signed PDF (at most 5 MB), its signer names, signed date and source reference, together with the complete dated rent, utility, pet, parking and concession schedule and deposit, pet, utility, parking, insurance, addendum and HAP obligations (explicitly “none” where absent). HAP terms name the housing authority, contract reference and exact tenant/HAP portions; PAY-013 owns posting and reconciliation of those payer splits. This is staff-recorded documentary evidence, never an assertion that Terrace collected or verified signatures (LEASE-011 supplies electronic execution). Each charge term and obligation names its source clause; amounts remain in the confirmed tenancy currency, never converted, and an unconfirmed historical currency must be confirmed under PORT-001 first. Versions take effect within the lease, after the last approved version; their signed original, payload and approved history never change. Preparation records no charge. The preview runs PAY-001’s property-local, inclusive-day proration and half-up cent rounding over the effective schedule; a concession reduces only rent covering its own interval. Approval binds the exact source, version, current tenancy, complete term snapshot and preview fingerprint and requires another approver under ROLE-003, with its sole-operator label. It atomically ends earlier terms the day before the effective date, supersedes unposted future terms and activates the prepared schedule; a changed source or terms needs a new version, and a competing version or posted period at/after the effective date refuses stale approval. The ordinary charge run then posts the previewed lines once, retaining the lease-version id and source clause in the resident ledger. Legacy add/amend-term commands cannot bypass a signed lease’s review, and a prepared signed version is later operating work that prevents silent import undo. Private sources and complete version data are available only to the owner and in-scope portfolio managers, bookkeepers and accountants; only the owner and portfolio managers prepare, preview or approve. Leasing staff read version dates, signers, references and obligation kind/date/source metadata without charge or HAP amounts or original downloads. Other roles cannot download signed sources; every source download is audited and no-store. SAFE-004 lease retention and legal holds preserve sources until eligible erasure, which removes originals and version text, remembers erased hashes and fences older archives; SAFE-005’s readable export contains original PDFs with hashes and restores full versions and terms. PORT-007 inherits broader searchable/versioned document custody, LEASE-012 approved renewal activation writes a versioned schedule, PAY-013 inherits HAP version/source links, and ACCT-003 inherits the versioned deposit obligation before those capabilities ship.

## PORT-006 — Complete move-in transfers and move-out

Staff record separate move-in and move-out operational checklists on each lease: keys, meter identifiers/readings with their stated units, condition references, possession and forwarding details. Each item is pending, recorded by staff with details, or not applicable with a reason; corrections append a dated revision with actor and source. Move-in is dated to the lease start; a recorded move-out uses its ended-on day. Changed lease/checklist versions refuse stale writes, and a changed move-out day marks an earlier checklist for review. A transfer starts with no destination checklist, so source keys or readings are never claimed for the new unit. Existing ROLE-001 move-out responsibilities and portfolio scope apply. SAFE-002 history, SAFE-004 lease retention/erasure and SAFE-005 readable backup/restore cover all revisions. These operational records alone neither post money nor assert deposit disposition or a reconciled final balance.

A charge names the resident, co-tenants and guarantors whose membership includes its due day: a leaver is named through their leaving day, a joiner from their joining day and never on earlier charges. `tenancy.transfer` carries every member who joined before the move-in to the new lease from the move-in day (listed `stay` members remain), closing their old place the day before, atomically. The record names people; it is not a legal opinion about who owes what.

## PORT-007 — Store searchable operating documents

Exact semantics (ct ruling 22 October 2026 18:00Z): a tenancy document is a lease or amendment, an application, a notice, a screening report or a tax document on one tenancy, at most 5 MB, in PDF, JPEG, PNG, plain text, CSV or .eml; a new version appends and the earlier one stays readable, each with its original bytes and SHA-256. The upload check is a static screen, never a malware verdict and never “scanned clean”: it refuses scripts, markup, executables, PDF actions, launches and embedded files, encryption and anything it cannot read, and a pass means only that the screen found no active content. Restricted kinds, always under ROLE-001 portfolio scope with 404 outside it: screening reports for the owner and portfolio managers; application documents also for leasing staff; tax documents also for accountants, who find, open and download them and no other document and never upload or revise one. A reader without a kind’s right finds no trace of it (404, like a missing record), and API credentials never reach restricted kinds. Every download is recorded in History and listed in the document’s version and download history. Retention classes follow the kind: leases stay with the lease (7 years after it ends), applications and screening reports 2 years after the last upload (LEASE-009 inherits decision-based periods), notices 3 years after the last upload (PORTAL-005 and PORTAL-006 inherit communication-based periods) and tax documents with accounting (7 years after their fiscal year); erasure removes the eligible classes and keeps each file’s hash as a re-upload fence. Documents stored before this ruling as inspection, work, invoice or other stay readable to holders of document.read; new ones attach to their own records under PORT-009.

## PORT-008 — Apply named state landlord-tenant rule packs

Each pack states, with statute citation, effective date, version and reviewer: security-deposit cap; separate-account, bank-disclosure and receipt requirements; deposit interest rate and payment schedule; refund and itemization deadlines; required disposition delivery method and address rule (personal delivery, first-class mail, certified mail); late-fee cap, fee basis and grace period; partial-payment effects on notices; pay-or-quit and lease-violation notice periods and permitted service methods; rent-increase notice periods and nonrenewal or termination notice periods; statewide rent caps and just-cause termination requirements (for example Cal. Civ. Code §1947.12 and §1946.2), with local overlays where a city adds its own; entry-notice period; required lease disclosures; and record-retention periods. A property whose state or overlay pack is not active, or whose pack lacks a field an action needs, is held from that deposit, late-fee, notice or lease action with a visible setup gap; no default value is ever substituted. Rule changes are new dated versions, and past decisions keep the version they used. Authority (ct 22 Oct): the owner alone publishes, activates and records counsel review. Every operator in a property's portfolio scope reads that property's active pack values, citations, versions and held reasons. A portfolio manager may propose a property's jurisdiction, and it takes effect only on owner confirmation. Content: Terrace authors, under a counsel engagement recorded with reviewer, firm and date, the 19 fields plus state lease disclosures and addenda and notice templates (pay-or-quit, violation, entry, nonrenewal, deposit disposition) for every pack. Each ships inactive until Terrace's counsel review is recorded. An owner may instead activate their own counsel-reviewed pack or template, recorded as theirs. Terrace-researched drafts carry citations and a verified-on date, and they never drive an action while inactive. Missing/inactive fields hold dependent previews without substitution; a preview changes no operational record and sends or moves nothing. A recorded reviewer is an owner attestation, not verification by Terrace.

ACCT-003/004, PAY-006, LEASE-010/012, PORTAL-009, SAFE-004 and BOUND-002 inherit reviewed, version-bound, missing-field gates before their pack-dependent actions ship.

## PORT-009 — Attach inspection media, work documents and invoices to their records

Exact storage semantics: photo/video originals up to 25 MB and other accepted documents up to 5 MB use 256 KB resumable chunks; finalization verifies the full SHA-256 and publishes one immutable version, keeping prior originals readable. Inspection media belongs to an inspection, work documents to a work order, and supporting invoice attachments to a vendor bill; supporting evidence never revises approved payable terms. Staff use existing work or bill rights, checked again on each request. A live vendor grant reads only current approved/sent assigned work and its own invoices at `/portal/record-media`, never inspection media; revoked sessions stop immediately. Originals are downloaded privately with a sandbox and no malware verdict or byte egress.

## PORT-010 — Scan uploads with the owner’s scanner

Scanner custody follows ct’s ruling of 22 October 2026 15:46Z: a verdict comes only from a scanner processor the owner configures for the installation (a self-hosted or owner-contracted scanning endpoint behind a Base adapter, with a server-held secret, audited configure, rotate and revoke, and recorded owner consent naming the processor); no document bytes leave the installation by default; bytes go only to that processor, over TLS, never logged, and Terrace keeps only the hash, verdict, scanner id and time; a scanner outage quarantines the upload (no download, a named reason, retry) and never passes it silently.

## ROLE-001 — Grant staff rights by responsibility

Exact semantics: each non-owner operator holds one Terrace role (portfolio manager, leasing, bookkeeper, accountant, maintenance coordinator or member) over Base’s sign-in tier (portfolio managers sign in as admin, the others as member) and covers either every portfolio or only the portfolios the owner assigns. The owner alone controls access (invitations, deactivation, roles and portfolio assignments, on Terrace’s and Base’s operator doors alike), integrations, money authorities (the PORT-001 currency confirmation; payouts when they ship) and cutover (move-in import, backup, restore and erasure). One capability table in D1 lists each role’s actions; every command, page tab, API read, download and export checks it for the signed-in operator, and every write rechecks it with the live operator and the role version inside the same D1 batch, so a role or assignment change applies from the next request (a change of sign-in tier also ends that person’s sessions). A record outside the operator’s portfolios answers 404 and never reveals that it exists; an action outside their role answers 403. A tenancy without a unit is reached only by the owner and all-portfolio staff, and installation-wide changes (the company, legal entities, operating accounts, new portfolios and tenancies without a unit) need all-portfolio scope. Portfolio managers manage tenancies, households, renewals and their approval, rent, charge terms and runs, statements, the rent roll and collections, properties and units, inspections, vendors and schedules, and approve maintenance within the owner’s per-property job limit and yearly budget (WORK-003), never access. Leasing staff manage tenancies, households, move-out, transfers, turnovers and renewal proposals, and never see ledgers, receipts, statements, collections, rent-roll money, operating accounts or EINs. Bookkeepers post charges, receipts and corrections, run lease charge terms, prepare arrears follow-ups, and read statements, collections, the rent roll, legal entities and operating accounts, never people or access. Accountants read every ledger and download every statement, rent roll and collections report, and post nothing. Maintenance coordinators run inspections, prepare, quote, reassign and hand off maintenance, record completions, assets, vendors, schedules and turnovers, and never approve spend, set owner limits, post money or change access. Members read their portfolios and prepare renewal, arrears and maintenance proposals. Admins at the upgrade became portfolio managers over every portfolio and members stayed members over every portfolio; an operator invited through Base’s own door gets that same default at first sign-in until the owner sets a role. Every role or assignment change is recorded with who made it and when. Doors not built yet inherit this before they ship: OPS-001 credentials, PORTAL-001 to PORTAL-010 staff screens, LEASE-005 prospects, ACCT-008, ACCT-010 and ACCT-018 payouts, PORT-007 and PORTAL-006 search, OPS-004 integrations, and ROLE-003 per-person approval limits and separate payment authority.

## ROLE-002 — Isolate residents owners and vendors

Exact semantics (ct ruling 22 October 2026 18:00Z, approved as adopted with riders): staff invite portal people (residents, delegated contacts, owners, vendors) with the fresh email-link proof, and owner and vendor grants need every-portfolio scope. Invitations and sign-in links are single-use and expire within 7 days. A session lasts 12 hours, and every request re-reads the live session, person and grant. A revocation, an ownership-date change (a new share set) and a work or invoice assignment change take effect on the next request and end the open sessions of the people they touch (the person revoked, the owners on either side of the change, the former and the new vendor), who sign in again by a fresh link. A delegated contact sees the lease’s balance and charges only, never screening, bank details, other households or documents, and the resident on the lease revokes them from their own portal page, recorded in history as theirs. An owner sees only the dated periods they own; after a sale they keep reading statements for the periods they owned, and nothing after. A vendor sees only approved or sent work assigned to them and their own invoices and originals. Portal people, grants, links and sessions are never archived: a restore carries no portal session or credential, and staff invite each person again. PORTAL-001, PORTAL-002 and PORTAL-003 inherit this door and add their own actions.

## ROLE-003 — Apply dual control and spending limits

Exact semantics: the owner’s per-job limit and yearly budget (WORK-003) and, beside them, an optional quote requirement (2 to 10 quotes when a job’s estimate is above a set amount) are recorded per property by the owner or a portfolio manager, labeled “recorded by staff for the owner”; the reserve requirement is the management agreement’s (PORT-003). Preparation, approval and payment are separate capabilities in ROLE-001’s table: members, coordinators and agents prepare; portfolio managers and the owner approve; nobody pays yet. Whoever prepared a maintenance job, renewal or arrears proposal never approves it, the owner included, and whoever revises a job’s terms, selects a quote or reassigns its vendor becomes a preparer of those terms; the approval answers 403. The one exception is an installation where no other active operator who did not prepare the terms holds the approval capability: the preparer’s approval is then kept labeled “self-approved: no second person”, rechecked inside the approval’s D1 batch. A job above the quote requirement cannot be approved with fewer quotes recorded. Any change of amount, payee (vendor), scope, title, due date or quote after approval makes a new version that must be approved again (with the owner’s decision again when over the limit or budget) before the packet or handoff (WORK-003). Payments, owner distributions, payee bank details and notifications of change are carried by ACCT-008, ACCT-010 and ACCT-018 (with SAFE-001’s step-up), which apply these rules before they ship.
