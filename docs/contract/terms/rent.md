# Rent collection and receivables: exact terms

The exact terms of the clauses in the “Rent collection and receivables” area of [CONTRACT.md](../../../CONTRACT.md). Each clause there is the requirement; the terms below define it.

## PAY-001 — Post recurring rent and lease charges

Exact semantics: lease charge terms (rent, utility, pet, parking or concession) hold an amount per billing period in the tenancy’s stored currency (USD for new tenancies, PORT-001) and an effective start and optional end. A monthly tenancy bills calendar months; a weekly or fortnightly tenancy bills 7- or 14-day periods counted from its lease start; a month’s run covers the periods that start in that month, so a period crossing a month boundary belongs to one run. A term covers the days of a period inside both the lease (its move-in and move-out dates) and the term; a partly covered period is prorated as amount × covered days ÷ period days, rounded half up to the cent, and falls due on its first covered day. A concession posts as a reduction of the same period’s effective rent and never exceeds it. If one concession spans multiple rent segments, its covered-period total is prorated and rounded once as above, then split by the segments’ covered days: floor each share and give remainder cents to the longest segment, earliest on a tie. Uncovered, overlapping or excessive concession allocations hold posting rather than omit the concession; zero-cent shares need no posting. A preview lists every line as new or posted and each period with no rent term as missing; posting requires the preview’s fingerprint, and each line’s reference (term and period start) is unique, so a retried or replayed run posts nothing twice. A term is amended from an effective date after its last posted period; posted charges never change. An owner or admin previews and posts. A renewal approval (TER-003) does not change terms; PORT-005 and LEASE-012 make a lease amendment or renewal write its terms here before they ship, and PAY-006 late fees and PAY-013 voucher splits post through the same run.

## PAY-003 — Collect rent by card

Card consent: a currently authorized full resident or the existing scoped rent-receipt staff role may explicitly consent to one hosted card payment. Delegated contacts and API credentials have no collection authority. The payer fee is zero and the property absorbs the actual processor fee. Terrace requests neither a saved payment method nor a mandate, disables adaptive currency conversion and Link, and currently refuses live credentials. A browser return never posts funds: the server retrieves and verifies the canonical provider event, exact reserved amount, intent and charge, then posts frozen allocations once. Provider fees and net are reconciliation facts, never evidence of a bank deposit. PORTAL-001 inherits this consent and authoritative receipt boundary.

## PAY-005 — Enforce partial and split payment policies

**Current semantics:** Owner-recorded lease policies order explicit charge ids and define increasing days-past-due stages. Admission uses property-local today and the oldest outstanding charge; both lease and every active PORT-008 partialPayments stage must allow a partial amount or credit. Missing/inactive packs hold admission. Pending external funds reserve charge capacity atomically without reducing receivables; settlement uses the frozen allocation once, failure releases it. Settled excess is separately identified unapplied credit, excluded from charge receipts and owner income; no credit is automatically spent. Policy snapshots retain exact pack versions. PAY-002/003/004 and PAY-013 inherit the same admission/reservation gate before their new collection doors ship.

## PAY-006 — Calculate late fees and grace periods

**Current semantics:** The operational calculator accepts confirmed USD monthly-rent charges explicitly identified in owner-recorded lease fee terms. Each active state/local pack must state its monthly-rent basis, cap, full unpaid grace days, weekendRule (calendar or next_business_day), explicit holidays, pendingPolicy (exclude_timely or count_pending) and reversalPolicy (hold or recalculate_once). Missing values hold execution. The lease may only lower the requested fee/cap, extend grace, move charging to the next business day or protect timely pending funds. The first fee day follows the full unpaid days after the due day, shifted by the explicit calendar; all applicable packs must allow it. Eligibility reconstructs unpaid principal on the first fee day (or the earlier schedule day), so later principal settlement does not erase an earned fee. Timely pending means initiated before that first fee day; later successful settlement retains that protection, and a failed timely pending payment follows the explicit reversal policy. A human reviews the exact fingerprint before posting. One source rent charge receives at most one fee; later recalculation under its original frozen policy may only reduce an unpaid, unreserved fee, never increase or compound it. A paid/reserved reduction is held for reviewed refund/reallocation; a waiver records its actor, day and reason.

## PAY-009 — Authorize refunds credits and write-offs

**Current external-record semantics:** Refunds, credits and write-offs have immutable full revisions with exact payee, amount, source, day and reason, before/after resident charge balance and owner ledger effects. Owner or in-scope portfolio manager prepares; ROLE-003 independence approves, with the sole-operator label only when no other active approver exists. A source, balance, allocation, reservation or authority change requires new review. Recording execution requires fresh email-link proof and explicit RECORDED confirmation of the external fact. Partial refunds reopen only the refunded receipt amount and reverse cash to its original frozen owners, proportionally to their remaining original receipt allocations in exact cents (floor each share, remainder in descending remaining allocation, owner id on a tie, capped at each owner’s remaining amount); originals remain unchanged. Credits/write-offs use the existing adjustment journal at the posting day ownership, reducing lease income or tax liability rather than claiming a cash refund. Check preparation records an approved numbered non-negotiable forwarding-address draft; external issuance is a staff-entered fact, never a print/mail, bank-clearance or provider-verification claim. No payment instruction is sent by these commands. ACCT-017/018 inherit the exact revision, live approval, limits and once-only source gate when their rails ship.

## PAY-010 — Issue resident statements and receipts

Requested financial-copy delivery: only the signed-in installation owner, with the existing email-link proof fresh within ten minutes, prepares a frozen document, authorizes an exact-recipient send or records provider evidence. Full resident grants must also match current PORT-004 household document rights in the property timezone; email requires the email preference. Delegates receive no expanded access. Record an affirmative resident request for the private link separately from the owner's exact-document send authorization, using consent version `resident-statement-link-v1`. The email contains only a sign-in link; the existing resident session and current rights protect the itemized document. Changed sources require new preparation. Authenticated provider callbacks bind the exact stored message and recipient; acceptance, mailbox delivery and owner-recorded provider evidence remain distinct. Failed or uncertain sends open a durable follow-up; no scheduler sends or retries, and accepted or uncertain claims cannot be sent again. Frozen financial documents and delivery evidence follow SAFE-004 accounting retention for seven years after the preparation year and communications retention for three years, preserving the longer period and holds. SAFE-005 backup and readable export retain the evidence without recipient or sending authority on restore. PORTAL-001 and PORTAL-005 inherit these recipient, consent and delivery boundaries before their future doors ship.
