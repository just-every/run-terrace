# Balanced property ledger

Open **Rent & arrears → Open balanced property ledger**. The general ledger contains confirmed USD only; AUD history and unconfirmed currency are never converted. It records external facts and does not hold or move funds or claim bank reconciliation.

Each immutable entry has a day, one property, one beneficiary owner, an operating or deposit fund, optional resident/vendor, a source kind/id/reference and 2–100 debit/credit lines. Debits equal credits in integer cents. Each account belongs to operating, deposit or both funds. A fund-incompatible account, unbalanced entry or negative recorded cash balance for that owner/property/fund is refused atomically. Other owners, other properties and deposit funds cannot cover a shortfall.

Rent charges debit receivables and credit lease income (lease tax uses tax payable). Recorded receipts debit cash and credit receivables. Reductions debit the source income/tax account and credit receivables. Approved bill expenses debit expense and credit vendor payable; revising the bill appends a reversal before a replacement approval. Fee lines debit management expense and credit management-fee payable, including their recorded fee tax. These postings are in the source transaction. Dated ownership shares use PORT-003’s exact allocation and remainder rule, frozen in the entries. Historical sources without mapped ownership keep a stable `lease-owner:<lease id>` beneficiary key rather than inventing an owner relationship. Existing source rows are backfilled by the forward migration, even in an already closed period.

The owner extends the chart by adding an account. Codes/types/fund classification and existing entries are never rewritten. Bookkeepers and portfolio managers post/reverse within their portfolios; accountants read through the financial-report door; leasing staff, maintenance staff and members cannot open the general ledger. Chart configuration is owner-only.

Use **Record a balanced entry** for opening balances, external deposits, owner contributions and vendor expenses. Deposits require their resident and deposit fund and credit deposit liability, never income. Contributions credit owner equity; a vendor expense names its vendor. The posting day must have already occurred in the property time zone. A manual entry is balanced within the owner’s dated property share; it cannot transfer to a different beneficiary or fund. Financial corrections reverse an original, leaving it intact. Corrections of native sources use their source door; a duplicate receipt uses **Reverse duplicate receipt**, retaining its reason/reference and an exact inverse in every affected beneficiary ledger. The original and reversal remain visible in resident receipts, reports and general-ledger source links. A second reversal is refused. Owner allocation corrections retain the original receipt’s dated ownership and agreement, even after a transfer; a fee run reverses the original recorded fee and tax, including a prior waiver, with exact inverse rounding.

The trial balance and property/owner/resident/vendor/deposit filters show all matching source-linked entries with equal debit/credit totals. A closed month refuses backdated journal entries and receipt reversals. New month closes preserve a separate immutable snapshot of their general-ledger entries alongside the existing rent statement. Native backup/readable export includes journal and chart configuration; restore replays no payment. Resident-linked journal references follow SAFE-004’s retention, scoped access and eventual redaction while numeric evidence, links and reversals remain.
