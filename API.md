# Terrace commands

Voucher payer accounting uses `hap.remittance.record`, `hap.remittance.allocate`,
`hap.remittance.outcome`, `hap.change.prepare`, `hap.change.preview` and
`hap.change.approve`. The native Rent & arrears forms use these same commands.
`GET /api/hap-remittances?tenancyId=ID` returns completely scoped remittance
records and held remainders. `GET /api/hap-reconciliation?tenancyId=ID&asOf=YYYY-MM-DD`
returns separate payer and owner balances; `format=csv` downloads the same
reconciliation. See [voucher payer accounting](docs/hap-accounting.md) for
authority, original custody, exact approval and documentary receipt boundaries.

All reads and writes authenticate a current Base operator session. Send the session cookie privately, never in a URL. Mutations require `Origin` equal to the installation origin, including command-line requests. Revoking an operator or changing their sign-in tier invalidates their sessions. There is no anonymous or implicit agent authority.

ROLE-001: the Authority column lists the roles whose rows in the capability table (`tt_role_actions`, migration 0030) grant each command; the owner holds every command. Staff with assigned portfolios reach only tenancies on units in those portfolios: any other record answers `404`, and a command outside their role answers `403` before any record is read. Company, legal entity, operating account and portfolio changes and tenancies without a unit need all-portfolio scope. `operator.invite` (displayName, email, role, allPortfolios or `portfolio:<id>` fields), `operator.role` (operatorId, role, scope as for invite) and `operator.status` (operatorId, status) are the owner's alone and recorded.

ROLE-003: `maintenance.approve` and `proposal.approve` answer `403 preparer_cannot_approve` to whoever prepared the terms (a revision, quote selection or reassignment makes that person a preparer), unless no other active operator who did not prepare them can approve; that approval is labelled `self-approved: no second person` (`approval_label`, and `approvalLabel` in the approval snapshot). Above the owner's quote requirement, approval needs that many quotes (`409 quotes_required`).

SAFE-001: `entity.add`, `account.add`, `operator.invite`, `operator.role`, `operator.status`, `factor.require` and `factor.reset` also need Base's fresh email-link proof: the sign-in link that created the session, or `POST /auth/step-up` with `{"action":"credentials:mint"}` and then that link opened in the same browser, within 10 minutes; otherwise `403 step_up_required`. A person with a confirmed authenticator, or anyone signing in by email link while the owner requires a second factor, gets `403 second_factor_required` on every other door until `factor.verify` passes for that sign-in (or, if not enrolled, `factor.start` and `factor.confirm`). Identity-provider (OIDC) sign-ins count as passed.

`GET /api/commands` lists commands. `GET /api/state` lists the company, legal entities, operating accounts and tenancies (each with its `currency` and `time_zone`); `GET /api/state?tenancy=ID` adds its rent, household, proposals, inspections and maintenance. `POST /api/commands` accepts JSON `{ "command": "name", ...fields }` and returns `{ "ok": true, "result": ... }`, or a non-200 `{ "ok": false, "error", "message" }`. Uploads use multipart form data to the same URL.

PORT-006: `tenancy.end` and `tenancy.transfer` return `turnoverId` for the source unit’s make-ready board when WORK-013’s next-day vacancy prerequisite allows it. The board and lease transition commit together; an exact unit/move-out board is reused with its task history. Unit-less leases return null. Immediate successor occupancy returns null with an explicit make-ready follow-up notice; the lease still ends. `turnoverOutcome` distinguishes opened, existing, unit_not_linked and successor_occupied, independently of display wording. No deposit refund, task completion or unit-ready claim is inferred.

PORT-006: `move.checklist` records an operational handover snapshot for `tenancyId`, `kind` (`move_in` or `move_out`), `moveDate`, the reviewed checklist `version` (0 when absent), and the current `leaseVersion` from `/api/state`. For each prefix `keys`, `meters`, `condition`, `possession` and `forwarding`, supply `<prefix>State` (`pending`, `recorded`, `not_applicable`) and `<prefix>Notes` (up to 1,000 characters; details/reason required except pending). Supply `reference` (up to 500 characters). Meter notes retain the staff-entered identifier, reading and units; Terrace does not calculate consumption. The existing owner/portfolio-manager/leasing move-out authority and live portfolio scope apply; prepare API credentials cannot run this command. Each successful correction appends a revision and advances the tenancy version; stale checklist or lease revisions return 409 with nothing changed. Move-in uses the lease start; move-out is inside the lease and must match its recorded ended-on day when ended. A changed departure date makes older evidence need review. `/api/state?tenancy=ID` and `/api/v1/leases/ID` expose `moveChecklists`, with parsed items, revision, source, actor/time, `needsReview` and operational `complete`. Completing these items does not reconcile money or deposits. Revisions follow SAFE-004 lease evidence, appear in resident access and readable exports, and survive `.rer` restore.

OPS-003: workflow commands. `workflow.template` (owner or every-portfolio manager; `name`, `kind` leasing/renewal/rent/delinquency/unit_turn, the kind's trigger field `daysBeforeLeaseEnd`, `staleDays` or `minDaysPastDue`, optional `propertyId` and `minOverdue`, `steps` as an array of `{kind,title,dueDays,assignee?}` or lines `kind | title | due days | assignee id`, `contactLimit` 1–10 (required with a contact step), `escalateTo`, `reference`; `templateKey` and the reviewed `version` to append a version) and `workflow.retire` (`templateKey`, `version`, `reason`). `workflow.start` (`templateKey`, `subjectId`, `reference`) starts a run on a record that matches the trigger; `workflow.sweep` (Run now, owner or every-portfolio manager) uses the same atomic sweep as the hourly Cron Trigger: it starts matching runs once per record/template, escalates overdue steps and creates pending WORK-010 orders. It never sends, records a reply/evidence, resumes or closes a run. Each template is fenced on the person who saved its version still being owner or an every-portfolio manager. `GET /api/workflows` returns tick receipts to owner/every-portfolio managers, including skipped templates needing a configurer. On a run (`runId` and the reviewed `version` from `GET /api/workflows`): `workflow.step` (`reference` evidence; task and gate steps), `workflow.attempt` (`channel` email/phone/text/letter/in_person, `outcome` reached/no_answer/refused/uncertain, `reference` external reference), `workflow.reply` (`outcome` agreed/declined/unclear, `on`, `reference`), `workflow.resume` (manager; `reference`, `resolution` delivered/not_delivered after an uncertain result or unconfirmed send reservation, optional `assignee`), `workflow.resolve` (manager; closes an escalation, never completes) and `workflow.cancel` (manager; `reason`). Each kind also needs the existing action for its records (leasing prospect.respond, renewal renewal.reply, rent and delinquency arrears.prepare, unit turn turnover.task.update) and the run's portfolio scope (404 outside). A paused run answers `workflow_paused` and an escalated one `workflow_escalated` (409) with nothing recorded; a spent contact limit answers `contact_limit` and escalates. `GET /api/workflows` (workflow.read) returns templates and runs with state, durable `next_action`, due day, contacts, pause or escalation reason and the sequenced events. The workflow engine sends nothing itself; existing prospect and renewal email sends are recorded against matching live runs and the shared contact limit. The hourly unattended scheduler runs the declared Cron Trigger; API credentials cannot run workflow commands.

Charges and receipt references are unique; duplicate retries are refused without duplicating money. Current versions are returned in `/api/state`; stale approvals and changes fail. A prepared proposal has no effect until approval. Agents use member accounts to prepare, then hand the proposal/job ID to a person. Approval records the actual authenticated actor and time; sending a `role` field cannot grant authority.

### SAFE-004 resident retention

Owner only: `privacy.request` takes tenancyId, kind (access/erase), source; `privacy.review` takes tenancyId/requestId and returns retained classes, reasons and fingerprint; `privacy.hold` takes tenancyId/class (lease/accounting/screening/communications/all)/reason; `privacy.release` takes tenancyId/holdId/reason. `tenancy.erase` now requires requestId, the current reviewed fingerprint and exact resident email in confirm. Holds and period changes invalidate the review. `GET /api/resident-access?tenancyId=...` downloads that primary resident’s private access JSON. Erasure retains amounts/dates/links and close evidence and redacts identity only after the reviewed periods and holds permit it. Download a new backup for the restore fence.

### WORK-005 staff appointments (partial)

`appointment.book` takes jobId, startsAt/endsAt, accessStartsAt/accessEndsAt (YYYY-MM-DDTHH:mm±HH:mm matching the property zone), permission=granted, permissionReference, noticeReference/noticeAt, keysAccess, availabilityReference and staffId (defaults to actor). Rescheduling also takes previousId/reason. `appointment.confirm` takes appointmentId/version/party (vendor/resident)/outcome (confirmed/no_response)/reference. `appointment.cancel` and `appointment.no-show` take appointmentId/version/party/reference; no-show requires the arrival window to have begun. `appointment.reassign` takes a cancelled/no-show appointmentId/version, vendorId/reference and reopens exact work approval. Managers/coordinators record these; no send or resident contact is claimed. State work includes appointments, appointmentEvents and scoped appointmentCalendar; maintenance reports include firstConfirmedAppointmentAt and bookingHours.

`emergency.guidance` (owner, or a portfolio manager over every portfolio) takes risk (gas/fire/electric/water/habitability/other), guidance, optional immediateDanger and callInstruction (required for immediate danger and gas/fire/electric; must name 911) and approves the next version. `emergency.oncall` (owner, portfolio manager) takes portfolioId, primaryId and backupId. `emergency.report` (owner, portfolio manager, maintenance coordinator) takes tenancyId, risk, emergency (yes/no), channel (phone/email/in_person/staff_observed), reporter, description, optional assetId and reportedAt (ISO with UTC offset, not future); it returns guidance, guidanceVersion and, for an emergency with on call, alertOutcome (the mail provider's status). `emergency.escalate` takes emergencyId/version, to (backup/primary) and reason and re-sends the alert. `emergency.acknowledge` (only the case's named on-call person or backup) takes emergencyId/version/reference. `emergency.resolve` (owner, portfolio manager) takes emergencyId/version, fieldWork and residentFollowUp. `GET /api/emergencies` returns scoped cases, events, current guidance, on call, portfolios and eligible responders. The maintenance report adds openEmergencies, oldestOpenEmergencyHours and emergencies. No SMS or voice page is sent and nothing escalates on a timer.

`prospect.add` (owner, portfolio manager, leasing) takes propertyId, name, email and/or phone, source (own_site/marketplace/email/phone/walk_in/referral), reference, optional bedrooms, occupants, moveIn, budget, pets, notes and consent_email/consent_phone/consent_text (granted/refused/unknown); a matching open prospect in the portfolio returns review=possible_duplicate with duplicateOf. `prospect.review` takes prospectId/version, decision (merge/separate) and reference. `prospect.assign` takes assignedTo; `prospect.email` takes subject and body and sends one email to the prospect through the installation's mail provider only while email consent is granted, returning emailOutcome (the provider's status); `prospect.respond` takes channel and reference (refused on refused consent; nothing is sent); `prospect.stage` takes stage (showing_scheduled/shown/applied/offer/leased forward only, or lost with lostReason and note), on (YYYY-MM-DD) and reference; `prospect.consent` takes channel, state and reference; `prospect.erase` (owner) takes reason. Each takes prospectId and version. `GET /api/prospects` returns scoped prospects with sources, consent, preferences, retainedThrough and history.

### ACCT-007 private vendor bills

`GET /api/bills` and `/app?tab=bills` list bills/authority/revisions/payables and monthly recurring templates. ### Governed custom fields, reports and documents (REPORT-005)

`GET /api/custom-reports` returns visible field definitions, schema history, sourced values, subjects, saved report/template versions and generated-document links. The native builder is `/app?tab=custom`. All reads use the existing property/work role rights and portfolio scope. Custom definitions belong to one portfolio and one subject kind (`unit`, `property` or `owner`). Owner subjects additionally require the existing private owner-directory rights: owner, or all-portfolio staff with `owner.add` or `reports.read`. Custom expressions expose only custom fields; they cannot select or join financial or other system columns.

The owner and portfolio managers use these commands. Their command aliases inherit `property.add`'s existing role list; portfolio scope is checked before reads/writes and live authority is rechecked in the write batch.

- `custom.field.define`: portfolioId, entity, key (stable lowercase identifier), label, type (`text`, `number`, `boolean`, `date`, `select`), readRoles (JSON array of existing staff role names), optional required, min/max (numeric bounds), options (JSON array for select), reason. Returns id/version. `custom.field.revise` takes fieldId/version and the same schema terms and reason. Revisions must validate every current recorded value without coercion. Keys, portfolio and subject kind remain stable; a different meaning uses a new field.
- `custom.value.set`: fieldId, entityId, version (0 before its first value), value, source. Text is at most 2,000 characters; dates are real YYYY-MM-DD days; booleans are true/false; select values must be a recorded option; numeric values are finite decimals of absolute value at most 1 trillion, within the declared bounds. An optional value may explicitly be null/empty. Validation failures and stale versions write nothing. Every value retains source, schema version and an immutable version history.
- `custom.report.save`: portfolioId, entity, name, readRoles, spec (object or JSON), reason. `custom.report.revise`: definitionId/version, name, readRoles, spec, reason. The spec has columns (`{key,label,field}` or `{key,label,formula}`), optional filters (`{field,op,value}`, ANDed; eq/ne/gt/gte/lt/lte/contains), groupBy (selected column keys), aggregates (`{key,op,column}`; count omits column, other operations sum/average/min/max). Formula trees use `{field:"inspection_score"}`, `{literal:2}` or `{op:"multiply",args:[...]}`; add/subtract/multiply/divide take two arguments and numeric fields only, up to eight nesting levels. Arithmetic uses finite IEEE-754 decimal numbers without monetary rounding; missing operands yield null; numeric aggregates ignore null; count counts rows. Division by zero and results outside absolute 1 quadrillion are refused. Reports never infer missing values as zero.
- `custom.template.save`: portfolioId, entity, name, readRoles, body (plain text with `{{field_key}}` placeholders), reason. `custom.template.revise`: definitionId/version, name, readRoles, body, reason. Fields must be known and visible. Every merge placeholder must have a recorded non-null value when generating.

`GET /api/custom-report?id=ID&format=json|csv` executes the saved report under the reader's current scope and role, returning columns, rows, groups and per-row lineage (field/schema/value version, source and recorded time). Report/template definitions pin every dependent field's schema version; any schema revision requires explicit definition review/revision before a new run or generation (409). Allowed staff roles only restrict existing subject rights. The owner always retains access. Hidden dependencies are refused before evaluating columns, formulas, filters, grouping, CSV export or merges (403), and omitted from the catalog and historical definitions.

`custom.document.generate` takes templateId, version (the reviewed template version) and entityId; its alias inherits `work.read`'s existing role list. It freezes plain text, template version and source/schema/value lineage in an immutable document, returning documentId. `GET /api/custom-document?id=ID&format=json|text` returns that snapshot; it never regenerates from current values or a revised template. Reads check both the original allowed roles and the current template/field restrictions, including historical dependencies removed from a later template. A visibility change can therefore deny later access without rewriting the original document. Downloads are private, no-store; report/document reads and CSV exports are attributable history events. Definitions, schema/value versions and generated snapshots are included automatically in backup/restore and readable export.

### OPS-001 API credentials and the version 1 API

The owner creates credentials under **Data & access → API credentials** or with `credential.mint`. A credential acts as one staff role (ROLE-001) over the portfolios chosen at minting; its capabilities and portfolios are read from D1 on every call and every write rechecks them in its batch, so a revoked or rotated token fails at once (`401`), a record outside its portfolios answers `404` and an action its role lacks `403`. The scope is narrower still: `read` answers GET only; `prepare` may also run `maintenance.prepare`, `maintenance.revise`, `quote.add`, `renewal.prepare`, `arrears.prepare`, `correction.prepare` and `charges.preview`. Everything else is refused by default: approvals, owner decisions, period and access commands, credential and second-factor commands (`403 credential_cannot_approve` or `credential_scope`). Credentials never open `/app`, `/api/backup` or `/api/readable-export`, are never archived, and carry no session, so they never pass a second factor.

Send `Authorization: Bearer ttk_…` (the token is shown once; only its SHA-256 is stored). Every GET above works for a credential within its role. Writes are `POST /api/commands` with a JSON body and an `Idempotency-Key` header of 8 to 100 characters (`A-Z a-z 0-9 . _ : -`; no `Origin` header is needed because a bearer token is not an ambient credential): the same key and body replays the stored response with `idempotent-replay: true`, the same key with another body is `422 idempotency_conflict`, and a key whose first attempt never reported an outcome is `409 idempotency_in_progress`, never run again. History records the credential as the actor with its role version and scope.

`GET /api/v1` lists the resources; `GET /api/v1/{properties,leases,households,ledgers,owner-ledgers,documents,work,reports}` pages one resource and `/api/v1/<resource>/<id>` returns one record. Lists are scoped before paging, sorted by id, with `limit` (1 to 200, default 50) and the previous page's `next` as `cursor`; the response is `{data,next,limit}`, and `next` is null on the last page. `ledgers` need `ledger.read`, `owner-ledgers` need `reports.read` and a statement period (`from` and `to`), `documents` lists lease-version originals (`lease.source`), inspection photographs (`work.read`) and current tenancy documents under PORT-007’s kind and portfolio permissions, with their SHA-256 and download address. Stored document ids are `document-version:VERSION_ID`; restricted kinds remain closed to API credentials. `work` lists maintenance jobs and `reports` lists the report doors the role may read. People signed in with a session may use the same paths.

Existing LEASE-005 prospect email and LEASE-012 offer-send doors reserve live workflow contact slots in their original send transaction. A renewal send to several recipients consumes one attempt, keeps each source email event and outcome, and pauses on any uncertainty. Accepted is awaiting a reply, not confirmed delivery; known non-sends consume no contact. Sends made while paused or escalated are listed without changing workflow state or counts. In-flight reservations block workflow work and further excess sends, survive restart/archive restore and can be reconciled by a manager through `workflow.resume` with source evidence and a delivered/not_delivered resolution; the unresolved attempt consumes its slot. A late provider answer is retained without recounting, and a contradiction pauses for review.
